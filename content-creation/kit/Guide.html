Install

  1. Quit Final Cut Pro.
  2. Double-click Install.command in this folder (if macOS blocks it: right-click → Open). It copies the four folders into ~/Movies/Motion Templates.localized and never deletes anything of yours.
  3. Manual alternative: in Finder press ⇧⌘G, paste ~/Movies/Motion Templates.localized, then copy the folders inside Motion Templates into the matching Titles.localized, Transitions.localized, Generators.localized and Effects.localized folders (create them if they don't exist).
  4. Open Final Cut Pro. Everything is in one category, CRMA, in the sidebar of each browser (Titles, Transitions, Generators, Effects); inside it the templates are grouped (Slides, Lessons, Lower Thirds …).
  5. Fonts: the kit uses Schibsted Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono (free, open licence). If they aren't installed, Final Cut Pro substitutes Helvetica — the templates still work; pick any font in the Inspector.

Using the kit

  1. Intros are full-frame and timed to their music — see Music & sound. Edit the title, tagline, URL and the eight beat words (Word 1–8) in the Inspector.
  2. Slides are full-frame teaching slides with their own graph-paper background. Type into the Text fields in the Inspector; every colour is published. Icons on slides are image wells — drag any icon or image from the browser onto Icon 1, Icon 2 … to replace it.
  3. Query Block / Query Card: type your SAQL into Line 1 … Line 7 (leave a line empty to skip it; up to about 44 characters per line). The lines type themselves on during the build-in; stretch the slide to hold it on screen longer.
  4. Quiz: use two copies of the slide. Leave Show Answer on Hidden on the first (pause for viewers), set it to A, B or C on the second to highlight the correct option.
  5. Lower thirds, lessons and titles have the Animation pop-up (slide, fade, pop, drop — fast or slow, or None). Text with a block reveal wipes on behind a colour bar; its colour is Reveal Color.
  6. End screens go in the last 5–20 seconds of a YouTube video. Drop thumbnails onto the Video wells (or leave them empty and place YouTube's own end-screen elements on top). Become a Member and Membership Perks promote the academy; the button keeps pulsing.
  7. Effects change the clip itself: put Webcam Box on your camera clip (above the screen recording) and pick a Corner; Slow Push In slowly zooms a talking head; Drawing Sheet Frame and Figure Frame put a screen recording on the blueprint sheet.
  8. Screen and Camera titles hold your footage: select the title, then in the Title Inspector click the Screen or Camera well and click the clip in the timeline or browser. Or put Camera Square (Effects › CRMA › Screen Capture) on the camera clip and Screen with Camera Space on the recording under it.
  9. Arrows and elements go above a recording: drag one in, set Position, Size and Rotation in the Inspector. Arrows draw themselves, then undraw at the end.
  10. Transitions go on a cut; drag the edges to change speed.
  11. All templates use the Blueprint palette — paper #F4F7FB, ink #0C1B33, navy #0F2A5C, signal #2F5BEA, glow #3FC6DC — so pieces match out of the box.
