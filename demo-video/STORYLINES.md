# CRM Analytics Academy — 30 s project film: storylines

**Picked: A** — a stranger follows one real question from "I'll be asked this" to "I'm ready", and every screen on the way is the real site.

## A · One question, start to finish (picked)
Device: one real object across the product. Form: object-to-object chain.
A real interview question from the site — "How does SAQL differ from SQL?" — is drafted on graph paper, then carried into the site's own Q&A page, and from there through the curriculum, the same lesson in four languages, a timed practice exam and the learner dashboard, before the whole site pulls back into one sheet.
- First 2 s: the question writes itself word by word in ink on the Blueprint grid; "SAQL" lands in signal blue on the downbeat.
- Beats: hook (the question) → reveal (it becomes the site's Q&A page) → learn → in your language → practise → track → payoff (every screen on one sheet, the real numbers land) → claim → end card.
- Look: the site's Blueprint sheet — paper #F4F7FB, 40 px graph grid, ink #0C1B33, one accent #2F5BEA, hard 6 px offset shadows, Schibsted Grotesk + IBM Plex Mono.
- Sound: the CRMA Lesson Bed (120 BPM), cuts on bars; whoosh on carries, click on the exam's Begin, pops as numbers land, riser into the logo hit.

## B · Twelve languages
Device: type carries the argument. Form: one frame that rebuilds.
One lesson stays on screen while its words roll through twelve languages like a slot machine; each roll rebuilds the page around it.

## C · The drawing sheet (the bold one)
Device: the object is the explanation. Form: a flight through one world.
The site is drafted as an engineering drawing — dimension lines, section marks, a title block — and the camera flies across the sheet from one detail (the exam timer, the leaderboard) to the next.
