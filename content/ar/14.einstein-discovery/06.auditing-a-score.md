      seconds: 40
    - shot: "Join what actually happened"
      screen: "Join leads to opportunities on ConvertedOpportunityId → conversion flag per lead"
      say: "Bring in the outcome: did this lead become an opportunity, and did that opportunity close won. Now each band can be measured against reality rather than against the score's own confidence."
      seconds: 45
    - shot: "The test"
      screen: "Chart conversion rate by score band"
      say: "Conversion rate by band. If the score works, this climbs steadily from left to right. If the line is flat, the score is noise wearing a number. If it climbs and then flattens above seventy, the score distinguishes bad from average and tells you nothing about the top — which is precisely where the sales team is spending its time."
      onscreen: "Does the line climb?"
      seconds: 55
    - shot: "Compare against a trivial baseline"
      screen: "Conversion rate by lead source, beside the score bands"
      say: "Now the honest comparison. Chart conversion by source alongside conversion by score band. If source separates outcomes better than the score does, the score is adding nothing over a field you already had — and that is a result worth reporting, however uncomfortable."
      onscreen: "Beat the simplest alternative, or admit it"
      seconds: 50
    - shot: "Check the volume behind each band"
      screen: "Put the lead count on the same chart"
      say: "Put counts beside the rates. A band with nine leads showing a sixty percent conversion rate is a coincidence, and it will be quoted as evidence the score works. Rates without volumes are how a bad score survives an audit."
      onscreen: "Rates need their counts"
      seconds: 40

# مراجعة الحسابات(أ)

أدلة الأكاديمية تحمل `LeadScore` وحساباتها تحمل `HealthScore`. كلاهما وثق بهما ولم يتم اختبار أي منهما. هذا الدرس هو ذلك الإختبار، وهو أكثر ساعة قابلة للتحويل في القسم - كل شركة تعمل بها ستكون لها نتيجة كهذه.

::note{icon="i-lucide-search-check"}
**A score is a claim: higher means more likely.** هذا قابل للاختبار ضد ما حدث فعلاً والاختبار يستغرق أقل من ساعة والسبب النادر جدا هو أنه لا توجد صعوبة - بل هو أن النتيجة تصل مع بائع أو مع مسمّى مغادر، وقد كانت على اللوحة لمدة سنتين، وتشكيكها وكأنها تشكك الناس الذين يستخدمونها.
::

## النتائج الثلاثة، وما علاقة كل منها

::compare-cols
---
items:
  - title: "إنها تنبأ"
    subtitle: "The line climbs"
    icon: "i-lucide-trending-up"
    pros:
      - "Keep it, and now you can say how strongly rather than hoping"
      - "The audit becomes a periodic check for decay"
    cons:
      - "Still worth confirming it beats the trivial baseline"
    verdict: "Good outcome. Re-run it in six months."
  - title: "تنبأ في الأسفل فقط"
    subtitle: "Climbs, then flattens"
    icon: "i-lucide-chart-no-axes-combined"
    pros:
      - "Genuinely useful for filtering out the worst"
      - "Honest framing: a disqualifier, not a prioritiser"
    cons:
      - "It is being used to rank the top, where it has no signal"
      - "That is exactly where the sales team spends its time"
    verdict: "The most common result, and the most useful finding."
  - title: "لا تنبأ"
    subtitle: "Flat line"
    icon: "i-lucide-minus"
    pros:
      - "Clear result, cheaply obtained"
    cons:
      - "Somebody built it, and people have been routing work with it"
      - "The conversation is political, not analytical"
    verdict: "Report it plainly, with the chart. The chart does the arguing."
---

::warning{icon="i-lucide-users"}
**الجزء غير المريح ليس التحليل** يستغرق الأمر ساعة الصعوبة هي أن النتيجة التي لا تتنبأ عادة ما تُستخدم لإعطاء الأولوية لعمل الناس لسنوات، فالقيادة بالأسلوب والرسم البياني بدلاً من الخلوص، تقدم المقارنة بين خط الأساس الثلاثي كبديل، وتسمح للخط الوارد في الجدول بأن يُصدر القضية.
