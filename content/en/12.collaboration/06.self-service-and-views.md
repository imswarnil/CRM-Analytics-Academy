# Self-Service and Views

Self-service is not a feature you enable. It is a property a dataset has or does not have, and it is decided almost entirely by work done long before anybody explores anything.

## What makes a dataset explorable

::field-table
---
dataset: prerequisites for self-service
grain: one row per factor
fields:
  - name: "Business labels"
    type: "decisive"
    role: "key"
    note: "'Deal Amount', not 'Amount__c'. Someone hunting for revenue among forty API names gives up, and reasonably concludes the tool is for specialists."
  - name: "Hidden plumbing"
    type: "decisive"
    role: "key"
    note: "Keys, staging columns and internal flags removed from the field list. Fifteen meaningful fields invite exploration; sixty with keys among them repel it."
  - name: "Sensible default aggregations"
    type: "prevents errors"
    role: "measure"
    note: "Especially on rates. A self-service user who sums a utilisation percentage across twelve months gets 960% and no warning — and then distrusts the whole dataset."
  - name: "A documented grain"
    type: "prevents errors"
    role: "dimension"
    note: "'One row per opportunity per product' in the description. Without it, a self-service user sums a duplicated measure and produces a number they will repeat in a meeting."
  - name: "Faceting that behaves"
    type: "makes it feel alive"
    role: "dimension"
    note: "Clicking anything narrows everything sensibly. This is what most people mean by self-service in practice — not building lenses, but clicking a bar and seeing the screen respond."
---
::

::note{icon="i-lucide-link"}
**Section 4's field-metadata lesson is the prerequisite for this one.** Nearly every complaint that "people do not use self-service" is a complaint about metadata nobody maintained. The tooling is not usually the obstacle.
::

## Saved views

A **view** remembers a state of a dashboard — filters, selections, a date range — so a regional director returns to their region without re-clicking it every morning.

::tip{icon="i-lucide-bookmark"}
**Views are the quiet win of this section.** Three clicks every morning is a cost people pay silently and resent slowly. A saved view removes it, costs nothing, and materially raises how often a dashboard is opened. Suggest one explicitly when you hand a dashboard to somebody whose slice is always the same — most people do not discover the feature on their own.
::

## The honest limits

::compare-cols
---
items:
  - title: "Self-service works"
    subtitle: "Follow-up questions"
    icon: "i-lucide-check"
    pros:
      - "'Which segment drove that?' — click the bar"
      - "'What about EMEA only?' — use the filter"
      - "'Which accounts?' — the table is already there"
    cons:
      - "Bounded by what the dataset contains"
    verdict: "This is most of what people actually want."
  - title: "Self-service does not work"
    subtitle: "New questions needing new data"
    icon: "i-lucide-x"
    pros:
      - "Nothing — this is a build request wearing a self-service costume"
    cons:
      - "The data is not in the dataset, so no amount of clicking finds it"
      - "Promising otherwise sets people up to fail and then blame themselves"
    verdict: "Say plainly that it needs a build."
---
::

::warning{icon="i-lucide-message-square-x"}
**Do not oversell it.** "You can answer anything yourself" is untrue, and the person who spends twenty minutes failing to find a field that was never synced concludes that they are bad at the tool. Say what the dataset covers and what it does not — people are entirely reasonable about a stated boundary and demoralised by an unstated one.
::
