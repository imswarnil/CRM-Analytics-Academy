## What this section will cover

What happens when somebody clicks: faceting, selection, filters and navigation are not settings to leave on defaults — they decide whether a dashboard is a system or a page of pictures.

## Planned lessons

- **Interaction is design** — What happens when somebody clicks: faceting, selection, filters and navigation are not settings to leave on defaults — they decide whether a dashboard is a system or a page of pictures.
- **Faceting & filters** — Clicking one widget filters the others — the behaviour that makes a dashboard a system rather than a page of pictures, when to switch it off, and the reference widget that must never move.
- **Selection & initial state** — What a dashboard shows before anybody touches it, how selection behaves once they do, and the small decisions that stop every viewer repeating the same three clicks every morning.
- **Drill-down & navigation** — Links from a summary to a detail dashboard and into Salesforce records — the escape hatch that keeps an executive screen at nine numbers while still answering the follow-up.
- **Interview questions** — Faceting, selection state and navigation — with model answers, and the design judgement the questions are really testing.
- **Graded quiz** — Eight questions on faceting, filters, selection state and navigation.
