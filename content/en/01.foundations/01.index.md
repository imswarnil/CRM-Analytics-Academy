- **What it is** — A materialised analytics engine that lives inside Salesforce's security model — what that sentence actually means, what it buys you, and the four jobs it is genuinely the best tool for.
- **The three layers** — Data, design and intelligence — the structure this entire curriculum is organised around, what each layer owns, and the handoffs between them where projects actually go wrong.

- **The vocabulary** — App, lens, dashboard, recipe, dataflow, story, model, explorer, query — precise definitions with the distinctions that matter, because half of all early confusion here is vocabulary rather than concept.

- **Your first dashboard** — Hands-on: the Academy's 140 accounts, one lens, three widgets, and a dashboard that answers exactly one question — built the way every later build in this course is built.
- **Interview questions** — Ten questions a hiring manager actually asks about CRM Analytics fundamentals, with model answers — and notes on what a weak answer sounds like.
- **Graded quiz** — Ten questions across the whole of the datasets and modelling section — what the platform is, the three layers, datasets and grain, the pipeline, the vocabulary, and when the answer is 'use something else'.
