# Graded Quiz: Foundations

Ten questions across the whole section. Graded in your browser — the answers necessarily ship inside this page, because it is public open-source markdown, so the quiz is only worth as much as your honesty with it.

::tip{icon="i-lucide-target"}
**Use it as a diagnostic, not a score.** Answer everything, then re-read only the lesson behind each one you missed. Four out of ten with two lessons re-read beats ten out of ten with nothing learned — and the questions are deliberately weighted towards grain, permissions and staleness, because those three account for most real mistakes.
::
