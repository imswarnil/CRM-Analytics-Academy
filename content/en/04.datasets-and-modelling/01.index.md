## What this section will cover

Data preparation gets the data in; modelling decides what shape it takes. Grain, history and metadata — the three choices that determine which questions a dataset can ever answer.

## Planned lessons

- **A dataset is a decision** — Data preparation gets the data in; modelling decides what shape it takes. Grain, history and metadata — the three choices that determine which questions a dataset can ever answer.
- **Grain & fan-out** — Join the Academy's opportunities to their product lines, watch $13.4M become $36.9M with no error anywhere, then fix it two ways and learn which to reach for.
- **Designing a dataset** — Working backwards from the questions a dataset must answer to its grain, its columns and its filters — and knowing when to build a second dataset instead of widening the first.
- **Modelling patterns** — The shapes that recur — fact and reference tables, wide versus long, derived layers and bridge tables — and which to reach for when.
- **Dates & snapshots** — Date types and fiscal periods, why an opportunity record cannot tell you what your pipeline looked like in March, and how a snapshot dataset accumulates the history that makes trend analysis possible at all.
- **Field metadata** — Labels, currency, decimals, default aggregations and field visibility — dataset-level settings that fix a problem once instead of in every widget that ever uses the field.
- **Interview questions** — Grain, dataset design, snapshots and metadata — the technical screen for this role, with model answers.
- **Graded quiz** — Ten questions on grain, joins, dataset design, modelling shapes, snapshots and metadata.
