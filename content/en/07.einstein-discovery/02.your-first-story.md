# Your First Story

Ask the data what makes a deal win.

::lesson-hero{figure="pipeline"}

  - "Remove leakage"
  - "Read insights and improvements"

::

## Setup

| Step | Setting |
|---|---|
| Dataset | Closed opportunities only, one row per deal |
| Goal | **Maximize** `IsWon` = true |
| Type | Insights **and** predictions |

## Leakage: remove before training

`Stage` · `Probability` · `ForecastCategory` · `LossReason` · `DaysInCurrentStage`: anything only known after the outcome.

## Reading it

- **What happened**: single factors, with lift over the average win rate
- **Why it happened**: combinations of factors
- **Improvements**: what would raise one deal's predicted chance

::warning
These are associations, not causes. Use them to ask better questions.
::
