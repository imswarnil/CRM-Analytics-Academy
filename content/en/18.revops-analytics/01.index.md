## What this section will cover

The half of go-to-market most dashboards forget: forecasting, the ARR waterfall, idle seats and unused prepaid pools, churn, learner support, Ask AI deflection, and the executive board that ties it all together.

## Planned lessons

- **RevOps & Retention** — The half of go-to-market most dashboards forget: forecasting, the ARR waterfall, idle seats and unused prepaid pools, churn, learner support, Ask AI deflection, and the executive board that ties it all together.
- **Forecast & Commit** — Build three forecasts rather than one, track forecast accuracy as its own metric, and handle the moment a number you published moves on a Thursday.
- **ARR Waterfall & NRR** — Build 14 — new, expansion, contraction and churn as a single bridge, and the one number that predicts the Academy's future better than any pipeline metric: net revenue retention.
- **Consumption & Risk** — Build 15 — seats used and prepaid pools drawn down against what was bought, under-consumption as the leading indicator of churn that arrives with no cancellation event, and whether overage revenue is worth celebrating.
- **Service, SLA & Deflection** — Build 16 — SLA attainment joined to ARR, answering 'was it us' with platform health, the CSAT response-rate trap, and measuring Ask AI deflection honestly rather than flatteringly.
- **The Executive Board** — Build 17 — nine numbers, one screen, no scrolling. The hardest build in the course, and the smallest, because by now sixteen dashboards have stakeholders who would all like to be on it.
- **Interview questions** — Retention, forecasting, idle-seat risk, service and executive design — the questions that distinguish a senior go-to-market analyst, with model answers.
