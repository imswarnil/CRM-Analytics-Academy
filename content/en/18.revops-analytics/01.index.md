# RevOps, Service and Retention

At the Academy, **a signed team deal is a promise, not revenue.** Forty seats bought and twelve activated renews at twelve; a classroom block left half-used does not renew at all. Growth from the existing base — $1.8M of expansion in the last twelve months — and losses from it both arrive without a lead or an opportunity. Everything in this section is about the period after the signature.

::note{icon="i-lucide-repeat"}
**Why this section exists.** A funnel-only view of a business that sells into teams misses its second-largest growth source and its largest risk. Expansion arrives with no session, no lead and no new-business opportunity; contraction arrives as idle seats with no churn event. Neither is visible anywhere in the demand or pipeline sections.
::

## The CRM Analytics you will use here

The post-sale half leans hardest on two ideas from earlier sections — snapshots, and never averaging a rate. Both are worth re-reading before you start if they are not automatic yet.
