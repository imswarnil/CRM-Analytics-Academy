  - q: "When would you use a subscription rather than a dashboard?"
    a: "For people who need a number routinely but won't open a dashboard: a weekly widget email. Exceptions get notifications instead, so routine mail doesn't train people to ignore alerts."
  - q: "What is the risk with threshold alerts?"
    a: "Alert fatigue: thresholds set too low, or on noisy measures, fire constantly and get ignored. Agree thresholds with the owner and review how often they fire."

# Subscriptions, Notifications and Actions

Push the number to people, and let them act on it.

::lesson-hero{figure="dashboard"}
---
points:
  - "Subscribe people to widgets"
  - "Alert on thresholds"
  - "Fix records from a table"
---
::

| Feature | When it fires | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| **Subscription** | On a schedule | Routine numbers |
| **Notification** | When a condition is met | Exceptions |
| **Action** | When a user clicks | Fixing a record in place |

::note
A record edited through an action is saved instantly, but the dashboard shows it after the next sync and recipe run.
::
