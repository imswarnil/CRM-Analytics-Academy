# Salesforce Foundations

Every CRM Analytics dashboard is a picture of Salesforce data. If you know how that data is shaped — which objects exist, how they point at each other, where the numbers really live — the dashboards are easy. If you do not, every chart is a guess that happens to render.

This section is the ground floor. It is short, it is practical, and nothing later works without it.

## What this section covers

1. **Objects, fields and relationships** — the data model behind every dashboard *(published)*
2. **Building a small app** — a custom object, a page layout and a list view, so you have data you understand
3. **Integration basics** — how data gets into Salesforce: imports, the APIs, and external systems
4. **Apex and LWC for analysts** — enough to read a trigger and an embedded component, and to know when you need one

## How to work through it

Read a lesson, then open a Developer Edition org and click through the same objects. Each lesson ends with a short quiz and the interview questions you are most likely to be asked about the topic. When the lesson has a video, it sits at the top of the page.
