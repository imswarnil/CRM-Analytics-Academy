## What this section will cover

What this section builds, the four ways data becomes a dataset, and the one question — what does one row represent — that decides whether anything downstream is right.

## Planned lessons

- **From source to dataset** — What this section builds, the four ways data becomes a dataset, and the one question — what does one row represent — that decides whether anything downstream is right.
- **Connections & sync** — Where data comes from, what data sync actually does, how to choose a schedule, and the dependency trap that makes a recipe run successfully on yesterday's data every single night.
- **Load a CSV properly** — Loading the Academy's opportunities and product lines, checking what Salesforce inferred rather than accepting it, and the three type mistakes that each cost a rebuild.
- **Buckets & binning** — Turning a continuous measure into named bands — where the boundaries come from, why bucketing belongs in the recipe rather than in each dashboard, and the edge cases that decide arguments.
- **Recipe nodes** — The node vocabulary — input, join, filter, formula, aggregate, transform, output — organised by the only property worth tracking through a recipe: what each one does to the grain.
- **Formulas & computed fields** — Computing per row versus computing after aggregation, where averages of averages come from, and which calculations belong in the recipe rather than in a dashboard widget.
- **Missing values** — Blank is not zero. How to tell a genuine absence from a real value, when filling a gap is legitimate and when it is a claim, and why missingness is sometimes the most useful signal in the dataset.
- **De-duplication** — Three different things get called duplicates — real duplicate records, join artefacts, and legitimate repetition — and only one of them should be deleted.
- **Troubleshooting** — A systematic method for wrong numbers: count rows at every node, match the symptom to its cause, and check upstream before touching the dashboard.
- **Interview questions** — Connections, sync, loading, bucketing, missing values and de-duplication — with model answers, and the operational judgement the questions are really testing.
- **Graded quiz** — Ten questions on connections, sync, loading, recipe nodes, buckets, formulas, missing values, de-duplication and debugging.
