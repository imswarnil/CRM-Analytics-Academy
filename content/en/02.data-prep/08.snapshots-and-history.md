# Snapshots and History

Salesforce stores the present. A snapshot recipe builds the past.

## The recipe

```text
academy_opportunities ─ Filter open ─ Formula SnapshotDate = today ─┐
                                                                     ├─ Append ─ Output academy_pipeline_snapshot (overwrite)
academy_pipeline_snapshot ─ Filter SnapshotDate ≠ today ────────────┘
```

First run: without the history input. After that, the recipe reads its own output.

## Rules

- Keep it lean: open deals, a dozen columns
- Prune: daily for 90 days, month-end after
- Schedule once a day, after sync

::warning
Never sum across snapshot dates. Filter to one `SnapshotDate` or group by it.
::
