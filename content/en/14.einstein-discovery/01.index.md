# Prediction, and Its Reasons

Einstein Discovery takes a dataset and an outcome column, finds what correlates with that outcome, and can deploy the result as a model that scores records and writes the score back into Salesforce.

::note{icon="i-lucide-split"}
**A story is an investigation; a model is a commitment.** A story tells you what correlates with winning a deal, in what direction and how strongly — useful on its own, and it produces no scores. A model takes that and scores live records, which means it starts influencing what people do. The gap between running a story and deploying a model is where most of the judgement lives.
::

## The rule that decides adoption

::warning{icon="i-lucide-scale"}
**A score without its reasons gets ignored within a month.** "This opportunity is 23% likely to close" tells a rep nothing they can act on, so they stop looking. "…and the three factors pulling it down are no economic buyer identified, 47 days since last activity, and a discount above 30%" is a to-do list. Einstein Discovery produces both. Deploying only the number is the most common way this capability arrives, gets distrusted, and is quietly switched off.
::
