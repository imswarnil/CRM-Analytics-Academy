## What this section will cover

What Einstein Discovery actually does, the difference between a story and a model, and the rule that decides whether any of it gets used: a prediction ships with its reasons or it does not ship.

## Planned lessons

- **Prediction & reasons** — What Einstein Discovery actually does, the difference between a story and a model, and the rule that decides whether any of it gets used: a prediction ships with its reasons or it does not ship.
- **What a story is** — How Einstein Discovery finds what correlates with an outcome, what the output genuinely claims, and the difference between a factor, a cause and a thing you can act on.
- **Preparing data** — The outcome column, leakage, grain and class balance — the preparation decisions that determine whether a model is useful, useless or confidently wrong.
- **Reading it honestly** — What an accuracy figure promises and what it does not, why a model can be accurate and useless, and the five questions to ask before letting a story change anybody's behaviour.
- **Deploying & write-back** — Turning a story into a model that scores live records and writes back into Salesforce — where the prediction should appear, what must travel with it, and how to monitor it afterwards.
- **Auditing a score** — The Academy already has a lead score and a health score, and everybody trusts them. This lesson tests whether they predict anything — the most useful hour in the section.
- **Interview questions** — Stories, leakage, deployment and auditing — with model answers, and the judgement that distinguishes someone who has shipped a model from someone who has trained one.
- **Graded quiz** — Eight questions on stories, data preparation, leakage, accuracy, deployment and auditing a score.
