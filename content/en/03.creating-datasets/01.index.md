# From Source to Dataset

Everything visible in CRM Analytics is built on a dataset, and a dataset is built by somebody making choices. This section is about making them deliberately.

## The question that governs this whole section

::note{icon="i-lucide-ruler"}
**What does exactly one row of the output represent?** You met this in section 1. Here you will build it wrong on purpose, see Namilio's revenue double, and then fix it — because the abstraction only becomes a reflex after you have watched it produce a plausible wrong number with your own data.
::

## The one habit to carry through the section

Count rows at every node.

::tip{icon="i-lucide-hash"}
**Before a join and after it.** If the row count went up, the grain changed, and you must be able to say why. Sometimes it should — joining accounts onto opportunities keeps 677 rows; joining product lines onto opportunities legitimately produces 1,378. The failure is not the increase. The failure is summing a parent's `Amount` after the increase, which nothing in the product will warn you about because nothing in the product knows what you meant.
::
