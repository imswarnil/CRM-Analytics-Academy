## Two words

| Word | Means |
|---|---|
| **Widget** | What you see: number, chart, table, filter |
| **Step** | The query behind it. One step can feed many widgets |

## The first dashboard

| Widget | Step |
|---|---|
| Number: Won | Sum of `Amount`, `IsWon` = true |
| Chart: by region | Sum of `Amount` by `Region` |
| Table: top deals | Name, Stage, Amount, top 20 |
| Date filter | `CloseDate` |

::tip
Charts hide problems; tables show them. Check every widget in table mode before you share.
::
