# Graded Quiz: Setup and Access

Ten questions. As always, graded in your browser, with the answers necessarily inside the page.

::tip{icon="i-lucide-target"}
**The weighting is deliberate.** Four of the ten are about failures that are *silent* — the integration user's missing field, the null that fails closed, the admin locked out by their own predicate, the inheritance fallback. Silent failures are the ones that reach production, so they are the ones worth over-testing.
::
