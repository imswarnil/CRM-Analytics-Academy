# Build: Organic Search When Answers Sit Above the Links

The Academy ranks #3 for *"saql group by example"*. Clicks fell 40% anyway. Nothing broke — an AI Overview now answers the question above the links.

::note{icon="i-lucide-trending-down"}
**The old assumption was that rank determined traffic.** It no longer does. Rank determines *eligibility*; whether a click happens depends on whether the answer was already given. A dashboard reporting position alone will show a healthy green line while traffic halves.
::

## The metric that replaces rank

::metric-spec
---
items:
  - name: "Clicks per Rank Point"
    formula: "sum(Clicks) / count(keywords) at a given position band"
    grain: "Per week × position band × cluster"
    source: "seo_keywords.csv"
    watchout: "Compare like with like. Position 1 and position 8 have wildly different expected CTR, so the comparison is always WITHIN a band, over time. Falling clicks at a stable rank is the signal; it means the SERP changed around you."
  - name: "AI Overview Exposure"
    formula: "count(InAIOverview = true) / count(all keyword-weeks)"
    grain: "Per cluster"
    source: "seo_keywords.csv"
    watchout: "Informational intent is hit far harder (~56%) than commercial and transactional (~20%). Blending intents produces a company-level number that hides which content type is actually being cannibalised."
  - name: "Effective CTR"
    formula: "sum(Clicks) / sum(Impressions)"
    grain: "Per cluster × week"
    source: "seo_keywords.csv"
    watchout: "Impressions still count when an Overview takes the click, so CTR falls while rank holds. That divergence IS the finding — do not smooth it away."
---
::

## Build it

::lesson-steps
---
items:
  - title: "The divergence chart first"
    description: "Timeline, Week on X. Two measures: average Position (inverted axis, so up is good) and sum of Clicks. When the lines separate, the SERP changed. This single chart replaces most of an old SEO report."
  - title: "Invert the position axis"
    description: "Position 1 is better than position 40, so a raw axis draws improvement as a fall. Reverse it in the chart properties, or your dashboard will read backwards to everyone."
  - title: "Exposure by intent, not overall"
    description: "Group by Intent, measure the share of keyword-weeks with InAIOverview = true. Informational will tower over the others — that is the strategic finding, and it changes what the content team writes."
  - title: "The at-risk table"
    description: "Filter to high Impressions and a CTR below the band's expectation. Sort by Impressions descending. These are pages with demand you are no longer capturing — the highest-return rewrites."
  - title: "Join sessions by landing page"
    description: "web_sessions.csv shares LandingPage with seo_keywords.csv. Aggregate sessions per page per week first, then join — otherwise you fan out one keyword across every daily session row."
---
::

::tip{icon="i-lucide-triangle-alert"}
**Careful with that last join.** `seo_keywords` is weekly and `web_sessions` is daily, so joining them raw multiplies each keyword by seven. Aggregate sessions to the week, then join on `LandingPage` + week. Or keep them as two faceted widgets — usually the better answer.
::

::note{icon="i-lucide-lightbulb"}
**The two dashboards answer one question together.** Falling informational clicks plus rising Share of Voice on the same clusters means your explainer content is working — it is just being consumed inside an assistant instead of on your site. Neither dashboard can show that alone, and presenting them side by side is the most valuable thing you will do for the content team this year.
::
