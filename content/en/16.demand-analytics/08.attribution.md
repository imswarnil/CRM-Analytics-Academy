# Attribution — Choosing Which Lie to Tell

A Salesforce admin read a free SAQL lesson in March, saw an Academy talk at a Salesforce event in May, asked Claude which training providers to shortlist in June, clicked a paid ad in July, and their company signed a Team Plan and a classroom block in September for $180,000.

::note{icon="i-lucide-help-circle"}
**Which channel earned that $180,000?** Every answer is defensible and every answer is wrong. This lesson is about picking one on purpose and being able to say why.
::

## What the dataset gives you

Two stored opinions, and they disagree about 34% of the time. That disagreement is the honest content of this lesson.

## The four models, honestly compared

::compare-cols
---
items:
  - title: "First touch"
    icon: "i-lucide-flag"
    pros:
      - "Credits what created awareness"
      - "Stable — cannot be gamed late"
      - "Favours content, the free course, SEO, GEO"
    cons:
      - "Ignores everything that closed the deal"
      - "Over-credits whatever ranks best"
    verdict: "Use when the question is 'where should we invest to create demand'."
  - title: "Last touch"
    icon: "i-lucide-flag-triangle-right"
    pros:
      - "Simple, and what most CRMs default to"
      - "Credits what converted"
    cons:
      - "Systematically over-credits brand and paid search"
      - "Someone searching your name was already sold"
    verdict: "Use for short cycles. Dangerous for Enterprise — it will defund the top of your funnel."
  - title: "Multi-touch"
    icon: "i-lucide-git-merge"
    pros:
      - "Closest to how buying works"
      - "Spreads credit across the journey"
    cons:
      - "Needs every touch captured — the Academy has two"
      - "The weights are invented, and become the debate"
    verdict: "Requires infrastructure you probably don't have. Don't fake it with two columns."
---
::

::lesson-steps
---
items:
  - title: "Join opportunities to their originating lead"
    description: "opportunities.OriginatingLeadId → leads.LeadId. Only new-business deals have one; expansion and renewal have none, which is correct and must be excluded from any attribution chart rather than lumped into a blank bucket."
  - title: "Build the first-touch measure"
    description: "Sum closed-won Amount, grouped by the lead's FirstTouchChannel."
  - title: "Build the last-touch measure the same way"
    description: "Same measure, grouped by LastTouchChannel. Plot both series on one grouped column chart."
  - title: "Add the disagreement flag"
    description: "A recipe formula: FirstTouchChannel != LastTouchChannel. Now you can quantify how much of the picture depends on the model choice — at the Academy it is about 34% of leads."
  - title: "Write the model on the chart"
    description: "Title it 'Revenue by First-Touch Channel', not 'Revenue by Channel'. Without the qualifier the number gets quoted in a deck and becomes a fact nobody can trace."
---
::

::tip{icon="i-lucide-scale"}
**When the two models agree, you have found something solid.** The free course usually scores high on both, because learners find you there and come back there. That is a far stronger case for investment than a channel that only wins under the model you happened to pick.
::

## The unanswerable question, answered

Back to the VP asking for revenue by keyword. Here is the full response.

::lesson-slides
---
items:
  - kicker: "Step 1"
    title: "Name the ask"
    icon: "i-lucide-ear"
    bullets:
      - "\"You want to know whether SEO pays for itself.\""
      - "Get agreement on the real question first"
      - "The keyword request is a proxy for it"
  - kicker: "Step 2"
    title: "Say what breaks"
    icon: "i-lucide-unlink"
    bullets:
      - "The session was anonymous — no key"
      - "There were 4+ touches over 6 months"
      - "Any single number here is modelled"
  - kicker: "Step 3"
    title: "Offer three real things"
    icon: "i-lucide-gift"
    bullets:
      - "Clusters that precede a signup, by landing page"
      - "First-touch revenue for organic, labelled"
      - "Influenced pipeline where organic appears at all"
  - kicker: "Step 4"
    title: "Hand over the assumption"
    icon: "i-lucide-file-signature"
    bullets:
      - "If they still want one number, ship it"
      - "Assumption written on the chart"
      - "They now own the model, which is correct"
---
::

::note{icon="i-lucide-quote"}
**The sentence to keep.** *"I can give you a number, but I want the assumption printed next to it so nobody quotes it without the caveat."* Almost nobody objects to that, and it protects every other number you publish.
::
