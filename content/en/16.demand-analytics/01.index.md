## What this section will cover

Six dashboards covering everything before a lead exists: AI assistant visibility, SEO in the AI-Overview era, GA4 acquisition joined to CRM, and whether the spend is working.

## Planned lessons

- **Demand Analytics** — Six dashboards covering everything before a lead exists: AI assistant visibility, SEO in the AI-Overview era, GA4 acquisition joined to CRM, and whether the spend is working.
- **AI Search Visibility** — Build the dashboard that answers whether an assistant recommends you. Share of Voice with a real denominator, mention rank versus recommendation, citation sources, and why there is no benchmark to compare against.
- **SEO & AI Overviews** — Rank stopped being the metric. Build the organic dashboard that measures clicks-per-rank, finds the clusters losing traffic to AI Overviews, and separates a ranking problem from a click problem.
- **Acquisition (GA4)** — Wire GA4-shaped traffic into CRM Analytics, separate AI Assistant from Direct, and measure the conversion that actually matters at a product-led training company: session to first lesson started.
- **Regional Demand** — Where demand is, where revenue is, and why those two maps rarely match. Build the geo dashboard that survives contact with a sales leader who wants to open a region.
- **Campaign Performance** — Build the campaign dashboard that survives a budget meeting: leads through to closed revenue per campaign, why the two spend grains never reconcile, and how to rank channels without letting volume win.
- **Spend Efficiency** — The dashboard finance reads: blended and paid CAC, CAC payback in months, LTV:CAC, and the specific reason a business mixing seat plans, prepaid pools and one-off classroom revenue cannot use the textbook formulas unchanged.
- **Attribution** — Every attribution model is wrong. How to choose one deliberately, build first-touch and last-touch side by side in CRM Analytics, and answer the VP who wants revenue by keyword.
- **Interview Questions** — Rehearse the demand-side interview: measuring AI search visibility, SEO when rank stops predicting traffic, CAC when some revenue is one-off, attribution model choice, and answering a request that cannot honestly be built.
