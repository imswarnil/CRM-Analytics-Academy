# RevOps, Service and Retention

At Namilio, **most revenue growth does not come from new logos.** It comes from accounts consuming more than they committed to, and it can leave just as quietly. Everything in this module is about the period after the signature.

::note{icon="i-lucide-repeat"}
**Why this module exists.** A funnel-only view of a usage business misses its largest growth source and its largest risk. Expansion arrives with no session, no lead and no opportunity; contraction arrives with no churn event. Neither is visible anywhere in modules 8 or 9.
::
