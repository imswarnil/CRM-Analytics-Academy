interview:
  - q: "What is faceting and when would you turn it off?"
    a: "Automatic filtering between steps on the same dataset. I turn off receive for fixed reference numbers (company total next to a filtered value) and broadcast for charts people shouldn't filter by."
  - q: "What is a binding and how is it different from faceting?"
    a: "Faceting only filters. A binding takes a selection or result from one step and injects it anywhere in another: a filter, a measure, a grouping, a title or a SAQL value."

# Faceting and Filters

Clicks filter the dashboard, with no code.

---
::

| Tool | Does | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Faceting** | A selection filters other steps | Same dataset |
| **Broadcast / receive** | Turn sending or listening off per step | Per step |
| **Connect data sources** | Facet across datasets on a shared field | Linked datasets |
| **Filter widgets** | List, date, range | Like any step |
| **Global filters** | One panel for every page | Whole dashboard |

::tip
Faceting only filters. To change a measure, a title or a calculation, you need a binding.
::
