## Planned lessons

- **Why SAQL exists** — The query language behind every lens and every widget — what a stream transformation is, why statement order is the query plan, and the four things clicking genuinely cannot express.
- **SAQL** — Salesforce Analytics Query Language — a stream transformation where statement order is the query plan, the five statements that cover most work, and the three patterns worth memorising.
- **Functions** — The SAQL functions worth knowing, grouped by what they are for — aggregation, dates, strings, maths and conditionals — with the ones that behave unexpectedly called out.
- **Filters & nulls** — Where a filter belongs in the stream and why it matters, the inequality that silently drops unknown rows, and how to tell a missing value from a zero.
- **Grouping & windowing** — Grouping by all, share of total, running totals, moving averages and rank within a group — the family of calculations that need a row compared against its neighbours.
- **Across datasets** — Combining two streams in a single query — when it is the right tool, when a recipe should have done the work instead, and the grain trap that follows you into the query editor.
- **Debugging** — A method for the queries that do not error — narrow to one entity, count before and after each statement, and match the symptom to the statement that caused it.
- **Interview questions** — The query-language questions, with model answers — and the judgement the good ones are really probing for.
- **Graded quiz** — Ten questions on statement order, functions, nulls, windowing, cross-dataset queries and debugging.
