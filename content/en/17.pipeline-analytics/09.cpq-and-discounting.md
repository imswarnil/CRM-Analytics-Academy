# Build: CPQ, Discounting and Deal Desk

Every dashboard so far has been about getting deals. This one is about **what they cost you** — and it is the dashboard most companies never build, which is exactly why margin leaks quietly for years.

::build-brief
---
dashboard: "CPQ & Discount Governance"
audience: "Deal desk, RevOps, VP Sales, and finance quarterly"
cadence: "Monthly, and at every pricing review"
question: "Where are we giving away margin, and what is the approval process costing us in cycle time?"
decision: "Which discount thresholds change, and which approval steps get removed."
success: "Average discount stops drifting upward; approval days fall without discount rising."
datasets: [quotes.csv, opportunities.csv, opportunity_products.csv, products.csv]
notInScope: ["Individual rep discounting league tables — that creates gaming, not discipline"]
---
::

## The versioning decision that makes this possible

::note{icon="i-lucide-file-stack"}
`quotes.csv` keeps **every version**, not just the accepted one. 795 rows across ~386 opportunities. A system that overwrites the quote can tell you what you sold for; only a versioned one can tell you **how you got there** — and the negotiation path is where the pattern lives.
::

::metric-spec
---
items:
  - name: "Discount Creep"
    formula: "avg(DiscountPct) by Version number"
    grain: "Per version, across all quotes"
    source: "quotes.csv — all versions, not just final"
    watchout: "This is the one analysis where you must NOT filter IsFinalVersion. Every other chart on the dashboard should. Mixing the two is the main way this dashboard goes wrong."
  - name: "Realised Discount"
    formula: "(ListTotal − NetTotal) / ListTotal, final version only"
    grain: "Per opportunity"
    source: "quotes.csv where IsFinalVersion = true"
    watchout: "Only meaningful on won deals — a rejected quote's discount was never given. Filter to the opportunity's IsWon = true or you are averaging in prices nobody ever paid."
  - name: "Approval Cost in Days"
    formula: "avg(ApprovalDays) by discount band"
    grain: "Per band"
    source: "quotes.csv"
    watchout: "Approval days sit inside the sales cycle, so they are a velocity cost as well as an overhead. A 35% discount approved over 11 days costs the discount AND the eleven days. Nobody counts the second half."
---
::

## The four findings this dashboard produces

::lesson-slides
---
items:
  - kicker: "Finding 1"
    title: "Discount creeps per version"
    icon: "i-lucide-trending-up"
    bullets:
      - "~2.5 points per re-quote"
      - "A 4-version deal ends ~10 points worse"
      - "Fix: cap versions, or require approval to re-quote"
      - "Invisible without version history"
  - kicker: "Finding 2"
    title: "Discounting may not win deals"
    icon: "i-lucide-help-circle"
    bullets:
      - "Plot win rate by discount band"
      - "Often flat, or an inverted U"
      - "Heavy discounts cluster on deals already lost"
      - "Correlation, so state the caveat"
  - kicker: "Finding 3"
    title: "Approval days are pure cycle cost"
    icon: "i-lucide-hourglass"
    bullets:
      - "~6 days at 15%, ~11 at 40%+"
      - "Sits inside the sales cycle"
      - "A velocity lever nobody counts"
  - kicker: "Finding 4"
    title: "Thin margins discounted hardest"
    icon: "i-lucide-alert-triangle"
    bullets:
      - "Certification Vouchers: 35% gross margin"
      - "Discounted like the 84% Team Plan"
      - "Join products.csv to see real margin after discount"
      - "Some lines may be sold below cost"
---
::

::tip{icon="i-lucide-calculator"}
**Finding 4 is the one that gets escalated.** Compute post-discount gross margin per line: `NetUnitPrice` against the product's `GrossMarginPct`. A 25% discount on a 35%-margin product leaves roughly 13% — and on vouchers the Academy buys from the exam provider at a fixed price, that is a decision somebody should have made consciously rather than a side effect of a negotiation.
::

## Build it

::lesson-steps
---
items:
  - title: "Two datasets from one file"
    description: "A versioned view (all rows, for creep analysis) and a final view (IsFinalVersion = true, for everything else). Build both in the recipe and name them clearly — every mistake on this dashboard comes from using the wrong one."
  - title: "The version chart"
    description: "Group by Version, average DiscountPct. Five points. Simplest chart here, and the one that changes policy."
  - title: "Win rate by discount band"
    description: "Band the discount into 0, 1-10, 11-20, 21-30, 31%+. Win rate per band, from the joined opportunity. Expect a flat or inverted-U shape, and present it as a question rather than a conclusion."
  - title: "Post-discount margin"
    description: "Join opportunity_products to products on ProductCode. Compute effective margin after discount. Flag any line under a floor you agree with finance."
  - title: "The exceptions table"
    description: "Final quotes above the discount policy, with ApproverChain and ApprovalDays. Not to blame reps — to see whether the policy matches reality. If 40% of deals need an exception, the policy is wrong."
---
::

::note{icon="i-lucide-users"}
**Frame this dashboard as process, never as people.** A discount leaderboard produces reps who hide discounts in non-standard terms, and you lose visibility of the thing you were trying to govern. The exceptions table exists to test the policy against reality — say that out loud when you present it.
::
