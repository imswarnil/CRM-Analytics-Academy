## What this section will cover

Five dashboards covering the machine between a lead and a signature: lead overview and routing, scoring, inbound versus outbound, the conversion handoff, opportunity management, funnel velocity, and CPQ discounting.

## Planned lessons

- **Pipeline Analytics** — Five dashboards covering the machine between a lead and a signature: lead overview and routing, scoring, inbound versus outbound, the conversion handoff, opportunity management, funnel velocity, and CPQ discounting.
- **Lead Overview** — The dashboard an SDR manager opens every morning: volume against target, coverage, ageing, and disqualification reasons read as a product signal rather than an excuse.
- **Routing & SLA** — Measure the handoff mechanics: routing coverage, SLA attainment, what happens to conversion as first-touch time slips, and how to prove a coverage gap rather than assert one.
- **Lead Scoring** — Audit a lead score instead of trusting it. Build the lift chart that shows whether score bands actually separate converters, and find the one field that outperforms the whole model.
- **Inbound vs Outbound** — The oldest budget argument in go-to-market. Build the comparison that uses conversion, cycle length and deal size instead of volume — and find the segments where each motion genuinely wins.
- **Lead → Opportunity** — The single most argued-about conversion in go-to-market. Build the handoff dashboard, reconcile marketing's number with sales', and measure rejection as a signal rather than a complaint.
- **Opportunity Management** — The dashboard an AE and their manager open every Monday: open pipeline by stage, stuck deals, the pilot and security gates that predict enterprise close dates better than stage does, and a real action list.
- **Funnel & Velocity** — Stage-to-stage conversion done properly with cohorts, the velocity equation and its four levers, and why the stage everyone worries about is usually not the one costing the money.
- **CPQ & Discounting** — Where margin quietly leaves the business. Measure discount creep across quote versions, the real cost of approval days, deal-desk load, and the non-standard terms nobody is counting.
- **Interview Questions** — Rehearse the pipeline interview — win rate denominators, cohort funnels, speed-to-lead causality, auditing a lead score, stuck-deal definitions, discount creep, and the MQL argument.
