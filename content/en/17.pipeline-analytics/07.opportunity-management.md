# Build: Opportunity Management

$21.8M of open pipeline, 402 open deals, and one question every Monday: **which three deals get attention this week?**

::build-brief
---
dashboard: "Pipeline Health"

## Filter to New Business first

::note{icon="i-lucide-filter"}
The Academy's 608 opportunities include Renewals that win 77% of the time. Leave them in and your funnel looks healthy, your win rate reads 40.4%, and none of it describes the work of selling. **Every chart on this dashboard filters `Type = New Business`, or splits by Type explicitly.**
::

## The metrics

::metric-spec
---
items:
  - name: "Pipeline Coverage"
    formula: "sum(open Amount for the quarter) / quota for the quarter"
    grain: "Per rep, per segment, per quarter"
    source: "opp_enriched + reps"
    watchout: "Say which quota. Rep quotas sum above the company target by design, so coverage against rep quota and against company target are different numbers. 3× is the usual rule of thumb, and it is only meaningful against a win rate — 3× coverage with a 20% win rate is not coverage."
  - name: "Days in Current Stage"
    formula: "today − StageEnteredDate"
    grain: "Per open opportunity"
    source: "opp_enriched"
    watchout: "Compare against that stage's own median, not one global threshold. Needs Assessment legitimately takes longer than Discovery, and a single '30 days is stuck' rule flags every healthy enterprise deal."
  - name: "Weighted Pipeline"
    formula: "sum(Amount × Probability)"
    grain: "Per quarter"
    source: "opp_enriched"
    watchout: "Stage probabilities are assumptions, not measurements. Derive them from your own historical stage-to-win rates and say so — inherited defaults from a CRM template are usually optimistic by 10-20 points."
---
::

## The gates that actually predict close dates

This is the part specific to selling training into enterprises, and the most useful thing on the dashboard.

::lesson-cards
---
columns: 3
items:
  - title: "SecurityReviewDays"
    icon: "i-lucide-shield"
    description: "9 to 87 days at Enterprise and Strategic. A deal in Negotiation with an unstarted vendor review is not closing this month regardless of what the stage says."
  - title: "PilotCompleted"
    icon: "i-lucide-flask-conical"
    description: "Pilot length runs 7 to 45 days. An open pilot past its expected window is the strongest single risk signal in the dataset — the technical champion has gone quiet."
  - title: "ContactsEngaged"
    icon: "i-lucide-users"
    description: "Single-threaded deals lose to 'Champion left'. One contact on a $400K opportunity is a risk flag even when everything else looks green."
---
::

::tip{icon="i-lucide-shield-alert"}
**Build a composite risk flag rather than six separate columns.** A recipe formula: at risk if days-in-stage exceeds twice the stage median, OR close date is past, OR the deal is in a late stage with no pilot completed, OR it is above $250K with one contact engaged. One boolean, sortable, and the action list builds itself.
::

## Build it

::lesson-steps
---
items:
  - title: "Compute each stage's median days first"
    description: "From closed historical deals, per stage per segment. Store it in the recipe as a lookup — comparing a live deal to a global threshold is the mistake that makes stuck-deal lists useless and ignored."
  - title: "Two views of the funnel"
    description: "Stage by count and stage by value, side by side. Many small deals in Discovery and three large ones in Negotiation is a completely different quarter from the reverse, and a single funnel chart cannot show it."
  - title: "Creation vs closure, on one timeline"
    description: "Pipeline created per month against pipeline closed per month. When creation drops below closure the next quarter is already short, and this is the earliest visible warning you get."
  - title: "Loss reasons over two quarters, not all time"
    description: "Loss reasons drift. 'Missing feature' concentrated in the last quarter is a product conversation; spread over two years it is background noise."
  - title: "The action list, last"
    description: "Values table on the composite risk flag, sorted by amount descending. Include NextStep — a blank next step on a large late-stage deal is itself the finding."
---
::

::note{icon="i-lucide-eye-off"}
**Keep this dashboard diagnostic, not evaluative.** The moment it becomes a ranking, reps manage the dashboard instead of the pipeline — deals get parked in Discovery and stage data degrades. Put leaderboards somewhere else and this one keeps telling you the truth.
::
