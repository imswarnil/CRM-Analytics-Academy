Not passing the context. The viewer selected a segment, landed on a detail dashboard showing everything, and has to reproduce the selection by hand. They do that once and then stop clicking. If the filters are not carried through, I would rather leave the link out. I also give detail dashboards a visible way home — people otherwise use the browser back button and end up somewhere unexpected.
