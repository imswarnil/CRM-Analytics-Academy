Widgets built on the same dataset filter each other by default, which is what makes a dashboard a system rather than a page of pictures — most people will never build a lens, but nearly everyone clicks a bar. I turn it off for a reference widget: a company total or a quarter target that must stay fixed while everything else filters, because if it narrows with the selection the comparison it exists for disappears exactly when it becomes useful. Also for widgets at a different grain — our platform health data has no account key, so faceting it by an account selection would imply a relationship the data cannot support.
