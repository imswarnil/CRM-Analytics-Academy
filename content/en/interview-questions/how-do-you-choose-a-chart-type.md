From the comparison the reader has to make, not from the shape of the data. There are six: rank, trend, composition, distribution, relationship, and one value against a target. Naming which one I am serving usually eliminates all but two candidates. 'I have a dimension and a measure so I'll use a bar' is how most charts get chosen and it is why dashboards end up as walls of bars.
