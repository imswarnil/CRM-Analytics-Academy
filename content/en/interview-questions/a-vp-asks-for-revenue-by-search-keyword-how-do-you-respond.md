I'd say I can't do that honestly and explain why in one sentence: the session that read the page was anonymous, there's no identifier linking it to the account, and the deal closed three or four months later through a different channel. Then I'd offer the nearest true thing — which keyword clusters precede a signup, and a modelled influenced-pipeline view with the assumption written on the chart. I try never to just refuse, because the VP has a real question underneath, usually 'is content worth the budget'. Naming what they asked for, saying plainly what makes it unmeasurable, and putting an honest alternative next to it usually gets taken. If they insist on the first version anyway, it now ships with a stated assumption and they own it, which is the right place for it.
