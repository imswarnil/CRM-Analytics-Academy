I'd build it, on a different dashboard. The moment a diagnostic dashboard becomes an evaluative one, reps start managing the dashboard instead of the pipeline — deals get parked in early stages, close dates get pushed in bulk, and the stage data that every other chart depends on degrades. So I'd keep pipeline health diagnostic and put ranking on a coaching dashboard with the fairness controls that ranking requires: filter or flag ramping reps, normalise by segment since quotas and deal sizes differ by a factor of ten, and use trailing twelve months rather than a quarter so one large deal doesn't decide the order. I'd explain the reasoning rather than just refusing, because the VP's underlying question — who needs help — is completely legitimate and better served by a dashboard designed for it.
