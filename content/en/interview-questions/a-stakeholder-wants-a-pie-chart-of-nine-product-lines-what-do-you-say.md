That people compare angles badly past about four slices, so nine slices will make them read labels — and once they are reading labels, a sorted bar or a table with a share column does the job better in less space. Then I ask what they do with the answer: if it is 'which are biggest', a bar; if it is the exact percentages, a table. I try to come back with the thing they actually wanted rather than a lecture about pie charts.
