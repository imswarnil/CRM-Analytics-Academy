I'd define a prompt list from the language prospects actually use on sales calls rather than from a keyword tool, cluster those prompts by topic and buyer stage, and run each one several times per engine on a schedule — five runs is enough — because assistant output is non-deterministic and a single check is an anecdote. From that I'd compute Share of Voice as mentions over total runs, and keep two more metrics separate from it: recommendation rate, which is how often we're the actual pick given that we were named, and citation rate, which is how often our own URL is the source. Those three separate different problems with different fixes — not being named is a content gap, being named but never recommended is positioning, and not being cited means our pages aren't structured to be quotable. I'd split everything by engine because citation behaviour varies enormously, and I'd be upfront that there's no industry benchmark, so the value is in the trend and in who gets named beside us.
