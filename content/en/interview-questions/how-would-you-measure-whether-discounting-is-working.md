Win rate by discount band, and I'd present it as a question rather than a conclusion. Often the relationship is flat or an inverted U, which suggests heavy discounts cluster on deals that were already lost and the discount was a rescue attempt rather than a lever. That's correlational and I'd say so. The analysis I'd be more confident about needs quote version history: average discount by version number, which at the Academy climbs roughly two and a half points per re-quote, so a deal that goes four rounds ends about ten points worse. That's invisible if the system only keeps the final quote. I'd also compute post-discount gross margin per line by joining to the product's margin, because a 25% discount on a 35%-margin voucher line leaves around 13%, and on a product bought from a supplier at a fixed price that may be below water. Finally I'd count approval days as a cycle cost, since they sit inside the sales cycle and nobody counts that half.
