Audit it, in about an hour. Bucket the score, join what actually happened after it was assigned, plot conversion rate by band with the volumes beside the rates, and compare against a trivial baseline like lead source. Three outcomes: it climbs, which is good; it climbs then flattens, which is the common one and means it separates bad from average while saying nothing about the top — where the team spends its time; or it is flat, which means it is noise. The hard part is not analytical, it is that the score has been routing people's work for years, so I lead with the method and let the chart make the argument.
