Four things written down and one person responsible: an owner per dashboard, the metric definitions, a short note on which datasets carry which security, and a habit of deleting what nobody opens. The deleting is the part people skip and it is the highest-leverage one — four pipeline dashboards with slightly different definitions guarantee an argument that correctness cannot resolve.
