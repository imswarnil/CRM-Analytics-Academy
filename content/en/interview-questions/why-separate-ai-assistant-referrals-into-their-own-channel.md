Because default channel groupings put them in Direct or Referral, and at a product-led company that buries the best-converting traffic you have. In our data assistant referrals convert to signup at around 4.9% against about 2.8% for organic, and they arrive with higher intent because they've effectively been recommended. If that's hidden inside Direct, nobody can make a case for investing in the work that produces it. Mechanically it's a formula field in the recipe matching the known assistant hosts, done once so every dashboard agrees. Two caveats I'd state on the dashboard: some assistants strip the referrer so the number is a floor rather than a total, and because the channel is growing from a small base it needs its own trend line — on a stacked chart it's invisible and gets ignored for another year.
