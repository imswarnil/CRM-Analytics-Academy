Four things. Whether it still predicts, by comparing predictions against what happened — decay is normal, because the business moves and a model trained before a product launched has a view of the old portfolio. Whether anyone acts on it, which is measurable and almost never measured. Whether the inputs have drifted. And whether using it is changing the data it learns from: if reps fill a field because the model rewards it, that field stops measuring engagement and starts measuring compliance. I also set a retirement condition at deployment, because a model with no retirement condition is never retired, it is just quietly stopped being believed while it keeps looking authoritative on the record page.
