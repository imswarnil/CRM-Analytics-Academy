Its top factors, in plain language, and the date it was scored. A bare probability gives a rep nothing to act on, so they stop looking within a month, and then when somebody asks why the investment changed nothing the answer is that it never produced an action. '23% likely to close' is inert; 'no economic buyer, 47 days since last activity, discount above 30%' is a to-do list.
