It is a signal first. Usually they are recreating something the dashboard does not do, and that is the most reliable source of genuinely wanted features I have. The real risk is the spreadsheet becoming a competing source of truth — a snapshot that never refreshes, gets edited and gets quoted six weeks later against a dashboard that has since been corrected. Nobody is acting in bad faith, which makes it harder to resolve than an error.
