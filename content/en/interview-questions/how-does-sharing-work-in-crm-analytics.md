The unit of sharing is the app, not the dashboard — an app is a container holding datasets, lenses and dashboards, with three levels: viewer, editor and manager. That makes app design a governance decision rather than a filing one, and it is why one app per dashboard is a bad pattern: sharing is maintained per app, so twenty apps means twenty lists that drift. I share to groups or roles rather than named users, because named sharing decays silently every time someone joins or moves.
