It weights an account with one committed seat the same as one with three and a half thousand. The correct figure is sum of used divided by sum of committed. It is the average-of-ratios trap, and in this data the two numbers differ enough to change what you would do. I also never roll utilisation across products with different units — messages and seats do not add.
