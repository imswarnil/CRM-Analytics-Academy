ARR is lagging: with a 97-day enterprise cycle, today's closed-won reflects demand from four months ago, so by the time the number moves the cause is cold and there's nothing to act on. Leading indicators sit upstream — share of voice in assistant answers, docs traffic, signup-to-first-API-call, speed-to-lead, stage conversion rates. I care because they're the only place intervention is still possible. The practical consequence is that I watch ratios rather than volumes: volume moves with spend and seasonality, but a conversion rate moves when something actually broke. If speed-to-lead slips from under a day to four days, lead volume looks unchanged and marketing looks fine, and pipeline dips a month later and gets blamed on demand. Only the ratio caught it.
