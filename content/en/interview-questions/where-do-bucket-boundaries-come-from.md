The business — the point at which somebody does something differently. Our under-55% utilisation band exists because that is where a CSM picks up the phone. Splitting a range into even thirds is arithmetic pretending to be judgement, and if nobody can tell me the boundary that is information: the measure may not be actionable yet. I bucket once in a transform node so 'at risk' means the same thing on every screen, and I write down which side the boundary falls on because somebody will eventually land on it.
