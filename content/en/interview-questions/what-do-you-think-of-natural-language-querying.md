Genuinely useful for getting to a first chart and for people who will never learn the explorer. The failure mode is that it answers fluently even when it has answered a subtly different question — the right measure cohorted on the wrong date, or a calendar quarter when the company runs a different fiscal one. So I treat a result as a starting point to verify in table mode, not as an answer to quote. Its quality also depends almost entirely on field metadata, so most complaints that it does not understand the data are really complaints about labels.
