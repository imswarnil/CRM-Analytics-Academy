When the computed value is a company metric — that belongs in the recipe, computed once, rather than recalculated for every viewer on every load in a place nobody can find. And when the binding exists to cram two audiences onto one screen; that is two dashboards wearing a trench coat, and splitting them makes both simpler to read and easier to share.
