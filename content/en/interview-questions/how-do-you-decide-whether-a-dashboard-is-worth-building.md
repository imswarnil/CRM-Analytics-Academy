Four questions before I open the canvas: who specifically opens it, when, what single question it answers, and what they do differently when the number is bad. The fourth is the one that decides it. If nobody can name the decision that changes, the dashboard will get opened twice and abandoned no matter how good it looks, and I've wasted a week. When a request fails that test I usually find the real dashboard is a different one — 'show me leads by source' is often really 'should I move budget', which is a spend-efficiency build. I'd also rather ship six widgets that change a decision than forty that document a department, because every widget I remove makes the rest easier to read.
