That they fire often enough to become noise. People build a filter rule, the alert goes to a folder, and the one time it mattered it is in that folder too. If an alert fires more than a few times a month the threshold is wrong or the thing it watches is not exceptional — and either way the fix is upstream rather than a louder warning.
