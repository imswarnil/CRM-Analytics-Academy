A raw total that needed a denominator. 'EMEA has the most support cases' is nearly always just 'EMEA is the biggest region' — the fix is cases per account or per hundred thousand of ARR. It is more common than truncated axes and it is harder to spot, because nothing about the chart looks manipulated.
