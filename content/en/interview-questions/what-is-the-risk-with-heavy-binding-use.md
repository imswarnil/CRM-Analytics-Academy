It is logic stored in widget configuration — unreadable at a glance, undiffable, and documented nowhere anybody looks. A dashboard with twenty of them works beautifully for its author and becomes untouchable for everyone else, so the next person builds a new dashboard beside it rather than risking a change. That is how an organisation ends up with four pipeline dashboards and an argument about which is right. The failure is organisational rather than technical.
