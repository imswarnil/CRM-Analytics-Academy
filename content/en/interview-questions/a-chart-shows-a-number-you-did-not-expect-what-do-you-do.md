Switch to table mode and look at the rows, before anything else. A surprising chart is either an insight or a grain problem, and the rows tell me which in about ten seconds — usually by showing me the same deal appearing twice. Then I read the SAQL to confirm the query is asking what I think I clicked. Changing the chart type tells me nothing.
