Sparingly, and never alone. Thresholds come from the business — the number at which somebody does something differently — not from splitting a range into thirds. Every coloured delta also carries an arrow or a label, because red-green is the common colour vision deficiency and colour does not survive a greyscale screenshot. And I set the direction of goodness per metric: churn falling is good, and a default palette will paint it red.
