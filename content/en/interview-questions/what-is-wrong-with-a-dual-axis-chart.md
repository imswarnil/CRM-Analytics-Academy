The point where the lines cross is decided by where you set the two axes, and readers interpret a crossing as an event. The two measures are in different units and cannot be compared at all. If the relationship is the question I use a scatter plot; if both trends matter, two charts sharing an x-axis. The one defensible dual axis is the same measure in two units, where the axes are locked together.
