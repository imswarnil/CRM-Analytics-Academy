I'd refuse to answer it company-wide, because neither wins overall — each wins a segment, and a blended answer is wrong whichever way it comes out. The deliverable is a segment-by-motion heat map coloured by closed-won: product-led dominates self-serve and SMB, outbound dominates enterprise and strategic, and mid-market is genuinely contested, which is why the argument always lives there. Before any of that I'd split product-led out of inbound, since a learner who worked through the free course is nothing like a webinar form fill, and lumping them together lets PLG's good conversion hide inbound marketing's weaker conversion. I'd compare on rate, deal size, cycle length and cost per closed dollar rather than lead volume. And I'd raise both teams' best objections myself — that outbound leads were often warmed by marketing first, checkable via first-touch channel, and that inbound would decay without brand spend, which is probably true and unmeasurable on this data.
