In three places. Sequence: a learner reads free lessons, signs up and works through a whole section before anyone knows their name, so usage precedes qualification and the 'funnel' is really a loop with a sales motion bolted onto part of it. Definition: a large share of self-serve signups never pay, so 'customer count' needs a rule before it can be charted, and finance, marketing and the board will each want a different one. And expansion: at a company selling into teams a lot of growth arrives with no session, no lead and no new-business opportunity — a team trues up its seats or buys another classroom block — so a funnel-only view misses the single largest source of net new revenue. I keep self-serve in the dataset rather than filtering it out, because the learner who pays $240 for Academy Pro is very often the champion who brings in the forty-seat team deal a year later.
