I'd ask which offering first, because the answer differs by pricing model and a single blended number would hide the interesting part. A seat plan like the Team Plan retains on a renewal date, so classic gross and net retention work — but I'd put seat activation beside it, because forty seats bought and twelve used is a contraction that has not happened yet. A prepaid pool of seat-days or consulting hours retains only if it gets used up; a customer can leave half of it idle without cancelling anything, so I'd measure it as draw-down against commitment, monthly, per account per offering. A flat programme fee is binary. And one-off classroom seats don't retain at all — for those the right metric is repeat booking. So I'd build those views and a blended one, and label the blend clearly rather than let it become the headline. The practical reason: on pooled and seat revenue, churn is a slow fade rather than an event, and a dashboard that only watches contract status sees nothing until renewal, by which point you've lost two quarters of warning.
