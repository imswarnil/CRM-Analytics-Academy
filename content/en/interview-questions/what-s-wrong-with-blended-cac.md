It averages things that shouldn't be averaged. At a company with a self-serve tier, a large share of new customers arrive at close to zero acquisition cost, which drags the blended figure far below what it actually costs to win a mid-market or enterprise account — so the number is true and useless for any decision. I'd publish it because boards ask for it, label it reference only, and put segment CAC beside it as the version you'd actually act on. I'd also check the paid CAC arithmetic carefully, because the most common error I see is dividing total marketing spend by only the paid-sourced customers, which mixes the denominators and understates CAC substantially. And I'd pair every CAC with payback months computed on gross profit rather than revenue — with margins ranging from about 35% to 88% by offering, using revenue makes payback look roughly twice as good as it is.
