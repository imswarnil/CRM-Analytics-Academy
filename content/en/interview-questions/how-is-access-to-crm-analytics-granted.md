Two layers. The org needs a CRM Analytics licence — Growth or Plus, with Einstein Discovery in the higher tier. Then individual access comes by permission set, not by profile: an admin and a user set per tier. System Administrator on its own grants no CRM Analytics access at all, which surprises people on day one. And access to the product is separate from access to content: a user can be fully licensed and still see nothing until an app is shared with them.
