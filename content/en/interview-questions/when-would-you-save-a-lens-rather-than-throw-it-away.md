Rarely. A lens is a disposable workspace — I expect to make twenty in an hour and keep none — and treating each one as something to preserve turns a fast investigative tool into a filing problem. I save one when it is going to seed a dashboard widget, and I name it for what it shows rather than for what I was doing when I made it.
