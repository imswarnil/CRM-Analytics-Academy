Anything that happens twice a year — the script rots, the credentials expire, and the annual task becomes debugging something nobody has looked at in twelve months instead of an hour of clicking. And design work: generating forty dashboards from a template gives you forty consistently mediocre screens rather than one that is right. I automate what is frequent or must be identical every time.
