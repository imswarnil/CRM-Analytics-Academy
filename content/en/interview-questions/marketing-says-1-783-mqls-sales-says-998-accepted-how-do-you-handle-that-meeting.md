I'd put both numbers on one screen with their definitions attached and the funnel between them, because half the conflict is that nobody has ever seen the two figures side by side with the cohorting rule stated. Both sides have to cohort by MQL date or the monthly rates can never tie. Then the diagnostic chart: rejection rate by source. If rejection is concentrated in one or two sources it's a targeting problem and you fix the channel; if it's uniform across every source, marketing's qualification bar is simply lower than sales' acceptance bar and you fix the definition. I'd also check whether rejection rate tracks leads-per-rep, because if it rises when the team gets busier, the leads weren't worse — capacity was — and that's a routing finding wearing a quality complaint as a disguise. The chart I'd really want is win rate by original score band on accepted leads only, which turns 'are these leads good' into 'does the score add information after the human filter'.
