By deciding what comes off. Roughly 130 widgets exist and nine things survive, and the work is not picking the nine — most people pick a similar nine — it is defending the refusals to the people who built them. My test per number is whether a twenty percent move would change a decision this quarter, and every chart on the board has to explain one of the tiles above it. Anything that needs a paragraph to interpret will be misread in a glance, so it belongs on its own dashboard with a link.
