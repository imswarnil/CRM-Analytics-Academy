NRR is what happened to the revenue from customers you already had: opening ARR plus expansion minus contraction minus churn, divided by opening, over the cohort present at the start of the period. New business is excluded deliberately — include it and you are measuring sales, not retention. It matters more in a usage business because most growth arrives without a salesperson: an account consuming more than it committed to expands with no opportunity, no session and no lead. And it compounds, which new business does not.
