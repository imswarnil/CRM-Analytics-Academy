Almost certainly the result page changed around us — most likely an AI Overview now answers the query above the links, or a competitor gained a rich result that pushed us below the fold. The diagnostic is to plot average position and clicks on the same timeline: if position is flat and clicks fall, the ranking is fine and the click has been taken, which is not something you fix by optimising harder. I'd check the AI Overview exposure flag by intent, because informational queries get hit far harder than commercial ones. Then the response splits: for commercial and transactional clusters it's worth restructuring the page to be the thing an answer has to cite, and for purely informational clusters the honest conclusion is sometimes that the click is gone permanently and the content should be measured by assistant visibility instead of sessions. That's an uncomfortable recommendation and it's usually the right one.
