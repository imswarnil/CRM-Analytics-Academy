Both tables carry AccountId, so it is provable rather than anecdotal. Take accounts with the churn-risk link on their cases and look at their ARR movements in the following months in the snapshot — contraction or churn appearing after the incident. I would also check the other direction, the accounts hit by outages that did not contract, because a claim that only looks at confirming cases is not evidence.
