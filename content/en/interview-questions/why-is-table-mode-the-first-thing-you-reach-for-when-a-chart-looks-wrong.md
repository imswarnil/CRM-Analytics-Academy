Because a surprising chart is either an insight or a grain problem, and the rows tell me which in about ten seconds — usually by showing me the same deal appearing twice. A chart renders a doubled total with exactly the same confidence as a correct one, so looking harder at the chart tells me nothing. Changing the chart type tells me even less.
