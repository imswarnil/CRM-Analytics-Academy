It carries the measure, the period, the filter and any limit — 'Top 10 accounts by ARR, Q3 FY26' rather than 'Accounts by ARR'. I assume every widget will be screenshotted into a message with no context, because eventually one is, and a title that survives that journey prevents a whole class of misreading. The limit in particular: without it somebody sums the visible bars and reports the total as wrong.
