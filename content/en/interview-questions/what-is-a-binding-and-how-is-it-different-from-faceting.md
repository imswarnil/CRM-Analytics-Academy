Faceting is automatic between widgets on the same dataset — click one, the others filter. A binding is explicit wiring: I take either what the viewer selected or what a query returned, and feed it into another widget's query, title or visibility. Faceting is behaviour you get; a binding is behaviour you configure, and pay for.
