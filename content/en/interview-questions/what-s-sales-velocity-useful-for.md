Less as a number than as a decomposition. It's opportunities times deal size times win rate, divided by cycle days, so when someone says 'we need more pipeline' it turns a vague demand into a choice between four measurable levers — and more opportunities is usually the slowest and most expensive of the four. The one people undervalue is cycle length, because it compounds: halving the cycle doubles revenue per day, and a lot of cycle time turns out to be process rather than selling, like approval days and unscoped pilots, which are cheap to fix. Deal size via multi-product attach is often the next cheapest. The critical caveat is never to compute it blended across segments — dividing strategic's deal size by self-serve's cycle length produces a number that describes no business that exists.
