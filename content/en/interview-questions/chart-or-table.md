Chart for shape, table for identity. If the question is 'which specific accounts', a table is the correct widget rather than a fallback — ten account names in a bar chart are unreadable and the names are the point. What I avoid is a table of every row with a scrollbar, which has stopped being a dashboard widget and become a data export; if people genuinely need the full list, that is a download.
