It is revenue and a risk at the same time, and I report it next to the renewal date for that reason. Sustained overage is unbudgeted spend on the customer's side, so it surfaces in their procurement review, and the usual outcome is a renegotiated unit price at renewal. Celebrating it in isolation sets up a surprise two quarters later.
