Because the explanation has to outlive the conversation. A verbal answer means the number's credibility depends on me being available, and an unexplainable number stops being used. Putting it where the number lives is how a dashboard becomes independent of the person who built it. My rule is that if I have explained something twice, it gets written down where it happened.
