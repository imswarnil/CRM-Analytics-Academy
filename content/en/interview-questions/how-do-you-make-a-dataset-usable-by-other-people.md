Field metadata, before sharing rather than after. Business labels rather than API names, formats set once, sensible default aggregations — especially on rates, because a utilisation percentage defaulting to sum reads as 960% across a year and renders cleanly. Hide the plumbing so the field list is fifteen meaningful items rather than sixty with surrogate keys among them. And the grain in the description, which is the one sentence everybody downstream actually reads.
