I'd build first touch and last touch side by side and treat the gap between them as the output, because the gap is where the actual decision lives. First touch credits demand creation and favours docs, content and organic; last touch credits conversion and systematically over-credits brand and paid search, since someone searching your company name was already sold. With a long enterprise cycle, running last touch alone will quietly defund the top of the funnel. I'd avoid multi-touch unless the touch data genuinely exists — with two stored columns, a weighted model is invented precision and the weights become the argument instead of the insight. Alongside both I'd show influenced pipeline, which is just 'of deals that closed, how many had any touch from this channel', percentages deliberately summing over 100%, because it answers 'would we miss this channel' without pretending to divide credit. And whatever I ship, the model name goes in the chart title.
