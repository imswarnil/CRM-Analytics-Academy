When the audience will not open a tool — most executives — or when the decision has a rhythm the delivery can match. The discipline is matching the rhythm to the decision rather than to the data: a daily email about a monthly decision trains people to ignore it, including in the month something actually moved. And a subscription needs a comparison, what changed, a link back and an owner's name, or it is a bare number nobody can act on.
