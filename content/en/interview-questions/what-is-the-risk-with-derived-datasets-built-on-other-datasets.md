A scheduling chain where a failure three links back is invisible at the end, and nobody can say which layer introduced a wrong number. I keep the chain shallow — base then derived — order the schedules with real headroom, and put shared definitions in the base layer so everything downstream inherits them by construction rather than diverging branch by branch.
