I'd want to rule out reverse causation first, because the obvious alternative explanation is that reps call the best leads fastest — in which case speed is a symptom of lead quality rather than a cause of conversion. The test is to repeat the analysis within a single score band, and ideally within a single touch-count bucket: if fast-touched A-band leads still convert better than slow-touched A-band leads, the effect is real and an SLA is justified. I'd also check whether fast touches cluster in one region or shift, which would mean I'm measuring coverage rather than speed. Once it survives that, I'd derive the target from our own curve rather than importing a number from a blog post, and present the curve alongside the SLA so the team sees where it came from — otherwise it's an arbitrary stick and gets gamed.
