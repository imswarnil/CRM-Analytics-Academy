Probably the denominator and definitely the mix. Win rate should be closed-won over closed deals only; if open opportunities are in the denominator you're counting live pipeline as losses. But the bigger issue is that it's blended across deal types — renewals win around 88% and new business around 33%, so a combined figure is mostly a statement about the renewal book rather than about selling. Then segment: self-serve wins about 44% and strategic about 24%, so the blended number is an artefact of this quarter's mix and will move when the mix moves, with nobody's selling having changed. I'd report win rate filtered to new business, split by segment, and on a trailing-twelve-month basis so quarterly noise doesn't drive conclusions.
