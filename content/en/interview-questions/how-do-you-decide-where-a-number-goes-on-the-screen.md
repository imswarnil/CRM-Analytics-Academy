Top left carries the headline, because that is where every reading order starts and it is the most valuable space on the page. The commonest mistake is putting the filters there — they are the first thing the builder configures, so they end up first on the screen, and the corner where the answer should be is spent on controls nobody needs until their second visit. Then proximity: widgets placed together are read as related whether or not they are, so I group by question rather than by chart type.
