The denominator, and whether it's segmented. Win rate should be closed-won over closed deals only; if open opportunities are in the denominator it's counting live pipeline as losses and understating the rate, which is the most common version of the error. Then I'd split it, because a blended rate across segments that differ by a hundred times in deal size describes no actual deal — at the Academy self-serve wins about 44% and strategic about 24%, so 33% is an artefact of the mix rather than a fact about selling. I'd also separate new business from renewal, since renewals win around 88% and quietly inflate any combined figure. And I'd check whether deals lost on 'timing' come back later as new opportunities, because counting the same demand as both a loss and a new lead distorts both numbers.
