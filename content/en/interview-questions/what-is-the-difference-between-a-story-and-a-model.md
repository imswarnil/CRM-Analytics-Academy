A story is the analysis: what correlates with an outcome, in what direction and how strongly. It produces no scores. A model is a deployed story — it scores live records and can write the prediction back onto the Salesforce record, which means it starts influencing what people do. The gap between running a story and deploying a model is where the judgement lives.
