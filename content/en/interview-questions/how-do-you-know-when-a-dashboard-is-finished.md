When the person it is for can make the decision it exists for, quickly, without me. Not when every requested widget is present. I start by writing the decision down — who makes it, how often, and what changes as a result — and anything that does not serve that sentence does not go on the screen. If I cannot write the sentence, I am arranging charts rather than designing a dashboard.
