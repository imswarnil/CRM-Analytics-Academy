Adding or removing widgets. The builder keeps the widget, its step and every layout consistent; by hand I would have to add it to each breakpoint correctly and the failure is silent — a widget present on desktop and simply absent on a phone. Mechanical, repetitive changes as text; structural changes in the interface.
