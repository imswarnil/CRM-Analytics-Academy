Three habits. One line per binding in the dashboard description, saying what feeds what. Prefer breadth over depth — five widgets bound to one filter is far better than three bound in a chain, even though it is more bindings. And name queries for what they contain rather than query_7, because binding references are the only readable part of the arrangement. The test I apply is whether a colleague could add a widget next month without asking me.
