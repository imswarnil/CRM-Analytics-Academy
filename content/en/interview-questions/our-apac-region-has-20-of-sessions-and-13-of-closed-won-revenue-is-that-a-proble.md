It's a question, not a finding, and there are four explanations with very different price tags. Coverage: demand arrives and nobody follows up, which I'd check with speed-to-lead and SQLs per active AE in region. Fit: the traffic is students learning for free, which shows up as disqualification reasons clustering on 'free course only' — headcount will not fix that. I'd also check the classroom data, because APAC carries 45% of classroom revenue, and a region that buys in person may simply not be buying online. Commercial friction: currency, payment methods, local entity, data residency, which shows up as losses on failed vendor review. And timing: if half that pipeline was created inside one sales-cycle length, the region should look bad because its revenue hasn't happened yet. That last one is the trap — comparing a young region's revenue to a mature one's is comparing a partial year to a full one, so I'd filter to deals created more than one cycle ago before anyone draws a conclusion, and put that filter on the face of the dashboard.
