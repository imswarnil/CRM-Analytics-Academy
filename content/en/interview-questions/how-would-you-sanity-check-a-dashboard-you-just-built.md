Against a number I can verify independently: a known total, a finance figure, a count someone else already published. Then I check the grain of every join, look at whether any total is a suspiciously round multiple of what I expected, and confirm what the refresh timestamp says. And I check it as a user with restricted access, because a dashboard that is correct for me and empty for the audience is a failure I would rather find before they do.
