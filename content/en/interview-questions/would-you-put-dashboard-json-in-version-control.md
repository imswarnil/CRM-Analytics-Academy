For production dashboards, yes — exported per dashboard, re-exported after significant changes, committed with a sentence about why. It is not deployment automation; the dashboards still live in the org. It is a record, and it is the closest thing to change control the product offers. When somebody asks why a number moved in March, the history usually is the whole investigation.
