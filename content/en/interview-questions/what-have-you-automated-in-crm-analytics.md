Mostly data loads and reconciliation. A nightly push of data that has no connector through the external data API, and a check that compares the dataset's total against the source system afterwards. The reconciliation is the part I would keep if I could only keep one — it turns 'check your work against something independently true' from a habit into a job that runs whether anyone remembers it or not.
