Five cases. Genuine real-time needs, because it refreshes on a schedule. A single-record view, because columnar storage is the wrong shape for that. Pixel-perfect documents. A one-off question, where a report is free and instant. And when most of the data lives outside Salesforce — at that point you are running a warehouse project with a CRM attached, and a warehouse with a BI tool on it will serve better. The question I ask early is what decision this drives and how stale it can be, because that surfaces the real-time case in week one instead of month three.
