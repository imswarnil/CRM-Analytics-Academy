Whenever the request is for substantial detail. Every 'can we also add' has two answers — add it, or link to the dashboard that already has it — and the second keeps the screen readable. Our executive board survives on nine numbers precisely because the other hundred-odd widgets are one click away. What I do not do is link to a destination with one widget on it; that should just be on the source screen.
