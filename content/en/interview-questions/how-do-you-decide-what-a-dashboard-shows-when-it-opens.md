Default to the common case rather than to everything. Current period, the viewer's own scope, sorted and limited to the top few — so the opening state is already the answer to the most common question. A dashboard that opens unfiltered and company-wide makes every viewer perform the same three clicks every morning, which is a cost they pay silently. And I exclude or clearly mark the partial current period, because showing an incomplete month beside complete ones manufactures a decline every month.
