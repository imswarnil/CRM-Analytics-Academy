A field recorded at or after the outcome — closed date, loss reason, a stage that only exists post-decision. It predicts the outcome nearly perfectly and is unavailable at the moment you would want a prediction. The tell is an accuracy figure that feels too good. The check is to ask of every column whether it would have been populated, with that value, at the point of prediction.
