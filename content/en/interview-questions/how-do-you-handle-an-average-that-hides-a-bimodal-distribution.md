Chart the distribution beside it. Our utilisation averages 88%, which is the mean of accounts at 30% — under-consuming and at churn risk — and accounts at 140%, in overage and heading for a renegotiation. Both need action in opposite directions and the average shows neither. I bucket in the recipe so the bands mean the same thing everywhere, chart the counts per band, and keep the average as a tracked number rather than as the answer.
