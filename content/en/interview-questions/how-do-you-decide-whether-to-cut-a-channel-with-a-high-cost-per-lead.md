I wouldn't decide on cost per lead at all, because it ranks channels by volume efficiency and says nothing about what closes. The example I'd give is conferences: worst cost per lead by a wide margin, and a disproportionate share of strategic pipeline. Cut them on cost per lead and you lose the seven-figure deals. So I'd plot cost per MQL against lead-to-opportunity rate to get four quadrants — cheap and good means scale, cheap and bad is the trap that lead-volume dashboards reward, expensive and good is worth keeping, expensive and bad is the cut. I'd add win rate beside pipeline ROI, because a channel can generate abundant cheap pipeline that never closes. And I'd set a minimum volume floor before ranking anything, or the top of the efficiency chart is a campaign that got two lucky leads and somebody tries to triple its budget.
