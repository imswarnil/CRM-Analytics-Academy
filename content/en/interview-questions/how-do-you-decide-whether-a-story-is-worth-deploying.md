I write the sentence: when this score is low, this person will do this differently. If I cannot fill it in with a real person and a real action, the story is worth reading and the model is not worth deploying. Most stories have top factors that are descriptive — segment, industry, company size — which are informative and unactionable, and a model made entirely of those changes nothing.
