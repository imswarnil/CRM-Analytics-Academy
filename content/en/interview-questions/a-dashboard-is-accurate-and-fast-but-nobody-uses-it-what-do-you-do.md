Treat it as a placement and ownership problem rather than a build problem. Is it where the decision is already made — embedded on the record page, in the Analytics tab, on screen in the meeting that already happens? Does it reach people who will never open a tool, via a subscription with a comparison and a link? Does anyone own it and is that name attached? A standalone dashboard competes with everything else in the day and mostly loses.
