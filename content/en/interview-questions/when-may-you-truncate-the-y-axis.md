On a line, where the encoding is slope and forcing zero on something that lives between 94 and 112 percent hides everything that matters — with the range labelled. Never on a bar, because bar length is the encoding, so truncating misstates the ratio between bars while every label stays accurate. The dishonesty is not the truncation; it is the truncation the reader did not notice.
