Net of reopens. A deflected case that came back was not deflected, it was delayed, and it cost the customer an extra round trip. I report gross and net side by side so nobody suspects the gap is being hidden. Reporting gross alone is the flattering version, and it is the version that gets an AI investment renewed on a false premise and then blamed when support load does not fall.
