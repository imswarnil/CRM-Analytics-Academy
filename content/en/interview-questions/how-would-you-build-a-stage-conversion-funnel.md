As a cohort, not a snapshot. A snapshot counts what's sitting in each stage right now, which isn't a conversion rate at all — in a growing pipeline it makes late-stage conversion look terrible simply because deals haven't got there yet. So I'd take opportunities created in a period and follow them, counting how many ever reached each stage rather than where they are now, because a deal currently in negotiation passed through discovery and counting only current stage understates every early stage. Then I'd exclude cohorts younger than one sales-cycle length, and do that per segment, since 28 days for SMB and 186 for strategic are completely different windows. And I'd build it twice — by count and by value — because the stage that loses the most deals is usually not the stage that loses the most money, and only the value funnel shows where revenue actually leaks.
