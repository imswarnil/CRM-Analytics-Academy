A materialised, columnar analytics engine that runs inside Salesforce and inherits its security model. You build datasets — snapshots rebuilt on a schedule — and explore them in lenses and dashboards, with Einstein Discovery adding prediction on top. The security inheritance is the part that distinguishes it from a general BI tool: who is allowed to see which records is already solved rather than rebuilt.
