The grain is what exactly one row represents. It matters most because nearly every wrong number in analytics is a grain problem: join 608 opportunities to 1,192 product lines, sum the opportunity amount, and $13.4M becomes $36.9M with no error anywhere — the join is correct, every row is correct, and the total is double. So the first question I ask of any dataset is what one row is, and I write the answer into the dataset description.
