A story is the analysis: what correlates with an outcome, in what direction, how strongly. A model is a deployed story that scores records and can write the score back onto a Salesforce record. The important part is that the prediction and its top factors travel together — a bare probability on a record page gives a rep nothing to act on and gets ignored within a month.
