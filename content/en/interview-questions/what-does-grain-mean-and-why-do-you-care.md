The grain is what exactly one row represents. I care because almost every wrong number in analytics is a grain problem. If I join opportunities to opportunity line items and sum the opportunity amount, the amount repeats on every line and total revenue roughly doubles — nothing errors, and the number looks plausible. So the first thing I ask about any dataset is what one row is, and I aggregate the many side before joining rather than after.
