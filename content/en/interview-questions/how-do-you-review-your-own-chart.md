I ask what somebody would conclude in four seconds and whether that is true — accuracy is the easy part and it is nearly always fine. Then a short checklist: does the title carry the measure, period, filter and any limit; does the axis start where it should; did a total need a rate; is there a distribution hiding behind an average; does the message survive greyscale. Then I ask a colleague who has not seen it what it says. If their conclusion is not mine, the chart is wrong even though every number on it is right.
