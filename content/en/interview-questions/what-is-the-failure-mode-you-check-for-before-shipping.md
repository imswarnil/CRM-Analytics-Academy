Half-wired faceting. A viewer filters to one region, four widgets narrow and three keep showing the global figure, and the screen as a whole is now false while every individual widget is correct. Nobody reports it because nothing looks broken. The test is thirty seconds: apply a filter and confirm every widget either changed or has a deliberate reason not to.
