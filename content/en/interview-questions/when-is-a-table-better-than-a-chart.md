When the identity of each row matters or the exact values do. 'Which ten accounts are at risk' needs the name, the ARR, the utilisation, the trend and the owner — five attributes, where a chart encodes two. The table is the correct widget there, not a fallback. What I avoid is a table with a scrollbar, which has stopped being a dashboard widget and become an export.
