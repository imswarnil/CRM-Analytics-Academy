Missing rather than duplicated still points upstream to permissions before the dashboard. Integration user field and object access first, then the predicate, then an inner join in the recipe that dropped unmatched records, then a filter narrower than intended. Duplicated is the opposite diagnosis — that is a join at the wrong grain, and I stay in the recipe for it.
