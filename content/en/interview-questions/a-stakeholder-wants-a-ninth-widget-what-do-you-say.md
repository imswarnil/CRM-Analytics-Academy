I ask what they would do differently depending on what it showed. Asked kindly that distinguishes a widget they need from one they are curious about, and curiosity is legitimate — it belongs in a lens rather than on the screen six people check every Monday. If there is a real decision behind it, something else usually comes off, because attention is the scarce resource, not space.
