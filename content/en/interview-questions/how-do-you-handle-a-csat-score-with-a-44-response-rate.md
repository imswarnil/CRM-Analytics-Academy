Publish the response rate beside it, permanently. The average is not wrong, it just answers a narrower question than its label claims — it describes people who chose to respond, and response skews to the delighted and the furious. A satisfaction score without a response rate is an advertisement; with one it is a measurement with stated limits, and stating the limit is what makes the rest of the dashboard credible.
