I'd say plainly that I can't build it honestly and explain why in one sentence — the session that read the page was anonymous, there's no identifier tying it to the account, and the deal closed months later after several other touches. Then I'd name what I think the real question is, usually whether content earns its budget, and offer three things I can actually build: which keyword clusters precede a signup by landing page, first-touch revenue for organic as a whole with the model labelled, and influenced pipeline wherever organic appears at all. If they still want the single number, I'll ship it with the assumption printed next to it, because then they own the model, which is the right place for it. Refusing outright is the wrong move — there's a real question underneath and the relationship matters more than being technically correct at somebody.
