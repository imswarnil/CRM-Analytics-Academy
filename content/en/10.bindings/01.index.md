## What this section will cover

Faceting is automatic; a binding is deliberate. What one widget feeding another's query buys you, the two kinds, and the maintenance cost you are agreeing to.

## Planned lessons

- **What a binding is** — Faceting is automatic; a binding is deliberate. What one widget feeding another's query buys you, the two kinds, and the maintenance cost you are agreeing to.
- **Bindings** — One widget's state feeding another's query — dynamic titles, dependent filters and conditional display, plus the point at which a bound dashboard becomes something nobody can safely change.
- **Selection bindings** — Reading what the viewer picked and writing it into another widget — dynamic titles, dependent filters and measure toggles, plus the empty state that decides whether the dashboard is usable.
- **Results bindings** — Feeding one widget with another's computed result — comparisons against a company average, dynamic thresholds and top-N context — and the execution order that makes dashboards slow.
- **Nesting & limits** — Chained bindings, the point at which a dashboard stops being maintainable, and the three habits that keep a heavily bound screen workable by somebody other than its author.
- **Interview questions** — Selection versus results bindings, execution order and maintainability — with model answers, and what the question is really testing.
- **Graded quiz** — Eight questions on selection and results bindings, execution order, edge cases and maintainability.
