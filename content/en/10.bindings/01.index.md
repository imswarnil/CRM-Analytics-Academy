# What a Binding Is

Faceting happens on its own between widgets on the same dataset. A **binding** is you wiring one widget's state into another widget's query, title or visibility — deliberately, and at a cost.

::note{icon="i-lucide-link"}
**The mental model: a binding reads something and writes it somewhere.** It reads either what the viewer *selected* in a widget, or what a query *returned*, and writes it into another widget's query, its title, or whether it appears at all. Everything in this section is a combination of those two halves.
::

## The two kinds

      - "Responds to interaction, which is what most people mean by a binding"
      - "Powers dynamic titles and dependent filters"
      - "Predictable — the input is visible on screen"
    cons:
      - "Needs a defined behaviour when nothing is selected"
    verdict: "Start here. Most useful bindings are these."
  - title: "Results binding"
    subtitle: "Reads what a query returned"
    icon: "i-lucide-database"
    pros:
      - "One widget can use another's computed value — a total, a max, a top category"
      - "Enables comparisons the click interface cannot express"
    cons:
      - "The input is invisible; debugging means tracing a query you cannot see on screen"
      - "Creates an execution order between widgets"
    verdict: "Powerful, and the first thing to suspect when a dashboard is slow."
---
::

## The cost, stated plainly

::warning{icon="i-lucide-network"}
**A binding is logic stored in widget configuration.** It cannot be read at a glance, it does not diff, and it lives nowhere anybody will look. A dashboard with twenty of them works beautifully for its author and becomes untouchable for everyone else: the next person can see that changing something breaks something, but not what or why, so they build a new dashboard beside it. That is how an organisation ends up with four pipeline dashboards and an argument about which is right.
::

The rule that keeps this manageable: **if a binding is not obvious from looking at the screen, write it down** — in the dashboard description, one line per binding, what feeds what and why.
