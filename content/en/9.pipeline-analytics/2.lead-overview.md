# Build: The Lead Overview

The lead overview is the most-opened and least-thought-about dashboard in most companies. It usually shows lead volume by source and stops there, which tells an SDR manager nothing they can act on before 10am.

::build-brief
---
dashboard: "Lead Overview"
audience: "SDR manager and the SDR team — opened daily"
cadence: "Every morning, and in the Monday pipeline meeting"
question: "Which leads need working today, and is the queue healthy?"
decision: "Who works which leads this morning, and whether to escalate a coverage gap."
success: "Ageing untouched leads trend toward zero; the manager stops building a morning list by hand in a spreadsheet."
datasets: [leads.csv, campaigns.csv, reps.csv]
notInScope: ["Revenue attribution", "Rep performance ranking — that is a coaching dashboard, built separately and deliberately"]
---
::

## The metrics that earn their place

::metric-spec
---
items:
  - name: "Untouched Lead Age"
    formula: "today − CreatedDate, where TouchCount = 0"
    grain: "Per lead"
    source: "lead_funnel"
    watchout: "This is the dashboard's reason to exist. An untouched five-day-old lead is nearly dead, and a volume chart cannot show it. Sort descending and put it in band 4 as a named list."
  - name: "Speed to First Touch"
    formula: "avg(DaysToFirstTouch)"
    grain: "Per source, per SDR, per day"
    source: "lead_funnel"
    watchout: "Use the median as well as the mean — one lead touched after 40 days moves the average and hides an otherwise healthy queue. And exclude self-serve signups, which have no SDR by design and would flatter the number."
  - name: "MQL Rate"
    formula: "count(IsMQL) / count(all leads)"
    grain: "Per source × week"
    source: "lead_funnel"
    watchout: "By MQLDate for the numerator and CreatedDate for the denominator, so recent weeks always look weak — leads have not had time to qualify yet. Either lag the chart by three weeks or shade the incomplete period."
---
::

## Build it

::lesson-steps
---
items:
  - title: "Start with the work queue, not the KPIs"
    description: "Build band 4 first. It is the widget that gets used, and building it first keeps you honest about what the dashboard is for. Values table, filtered to open statuses and TouchCount = 0, sorted by CreatedDate ascending."
  - title: "Bucket the ageing"
    description: "A recipe formula field with four buckets rather than raw day counts, so the chart has four bars instead of ninety. Under a day, one to two, three to five, five plus."
  - title: "Exclude self-serve from the SLA charts"
    description: "SelfServeSignup = true leads have no SDR and a DaysToFirstTouch near zero by construction. Leaving them in makes your speed-to-lead look excellent while the leads that need calling rot."
  - title: "Make disqualification reasons prominent"
    description: "Not hidden in a drill-down. This chart is a product and targeting signal, and it is the one marketing needs to see."
  - title: "Rate charts beside volume charts"
    description: "Leads by source answers 'where is the noise'. MQL rate by source answers 'where is the signal'. Put them side by side and the comparison makes itself."
---
::

::tip{icon="i-lucide-clock"}
**Speed to lead is the highest-leverage number here.** At Namilio, sales-assisted leads average about 2.1 days to first touch. Every published study of this metric finds conversion falling steeply after the first hour. If your dashboard changes one behaviour, make it this one — and show the median, not the mean.
::

## Read disqualification reasons as a signal

Most teams treat this field as admin. It is one of the most informative columns in the warehouse.

::lesson-cards
---
columns: 3
items:
  - title: "\"Student / hobby project\""
    icon: "i-lucide-graduation-cap"
    description: "A targeting problem, usually paid social or a viral post. Clustered in one region, it explains a conversion gap the regional dashboard could only raise as a question."
  - title: "\"Free tier is enough\""
    icon: "i-lucide-gift"
    description: "Not a lead-quality problem at all — a packaging one. Rising month over month is a product-pricing conversation, and you are the only person who can see it."
  - title: "\"Went silent\""
    icon: "i-lucide-ghost"
    description: "Often really a speed-to-lead problem wearing a disguise. Cross-check against DaysToFirstTouch before accepting it at face value."
---
::

::note{icon="i-lucide-eye"}
**Keep disqualified leads in the dataset.** Filtering them out raises every conversion rate on the dashboard and removes the only evidence of *why* leads are bad. A funnel that silently drops its failures flatters everyone and teaches nobody.
::
