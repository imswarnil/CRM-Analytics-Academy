# Transpose, Flatten and Split

Three transformations that change the shape of data.

## Transpose: wide → long

| AccountId | Month | New | Expansion | Churned |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ACC-1 | 2026-05 | 0 | 2,000 | 0 |

becomes

| AccountId | Month | Movement | Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| ACC-1 | 2026-05 | New | 0 |
| ACC-1 | 2026-05 | Expansion | 2,000 |
| ACC-1 | 2026-05 | Churned | 0 |

Long is for charts and filters. **Rows → columns** is Aggregate with **group columns**.

## Flatten

`User.Id` + `User.ManagerId` → a multi-value **path** of every manager above each user. Joined onto records by owner, it powers manager-level security.

## Split

`'VOUCHER;CLASSROOM;SEATS'` → three columns, by delimiter.
