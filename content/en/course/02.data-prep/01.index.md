# Data Prep

Dashboards are only as right as the dataset under them. This section is the data engineering: recipes, joins that never double a number, and history you can trend.

## What this section covers

1. **The data layer**: sync, connections, recipes, datasets
2. **Your first recipe**
3. **Joins in depth**: lookup, left, inner, outer, multi-value, fan-out
4. **Append and aggregate**: grain you choose on purpose
5. **Formulas, buckets and dates**
6. **Transpose, flatten and split**
7. **Snapshots and history**
8. **Security in the data**: predicates and sharing inheritance
