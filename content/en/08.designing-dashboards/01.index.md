## What this section will cover

A dashboard that supports no decision is decoration. The brief, the four-second test, and why subtraction is the senior skill this whole section trains.

## Planned lessons

- **Design for a decision** — A dashboard that supports no decision is decoration. The brief, the four-second test, and why subtraction is the senior skill this whole section trains.
- **Layout & hierarchy** — Reading order, grouping and visual weight — how a dashboard tells someone where to look first without anybody explaining it, and the three layouts that cover almost every case.
- **Conditional formatting** — Colour that carries meaning — thresholds that come from the business rather than from a palette, why colour alone is never enough, and the dashboard where everything is coloured so nothing is.
- **Tables, KPIs & sparklines** — The three widget families that carry most executive dashboards — when a table beats a chart, what makes a KPI tile actionable, and why a sparkline is worth more than a second chart.
- **Performance** — Why a dashboard takes twelve seconds to load, the query-sharing change that usually takes it to two, and the honest trade between pre-aggregating and staying flexible.
- **Hands-on: pipeline** — Build the Academy's pipeline dashboard end to end — a written brief, six widgets, three deliberately refused, and a check against the answer key before anyone else sees it.
- **Interview questions** — Design, hierarchy, colour and performance — with model answers, where the strong ones are about what you refused to build rather than which chart type you chose.
- **Graded quiz** — Ten questions on hierarchy, titles, conditional formatting, widget choice and performance.
