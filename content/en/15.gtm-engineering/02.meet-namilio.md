# Meet Namilio

**Namilio** sells the plumbing behind AI products: voice agents that answer phones, a messaging API, an LLM copilot endpoint, phone verification, and a studio where product teams wire it together. Roughly 140 customers, a few thousand leads, about $58M closed to date.

It does not exist. Everything else about it is real — the shape is copied from the companies that actually hire GTM engineers, and each odd detail exists because it breaks a metric you would otherwise trust.

::tip{icon="i-lucide-lightbulb"}
**The interview answer.** Asked "how would you measure retention here", the weak answer is a formula. The strong one: *"Which product? Seats retain on a renewal date, usage retains every single day, and the platform fee retains or doesn't. I'd build three and a blended one, and I'd label the blend clearly."*
::

::tip{icon="i-lucide-users"}
**Use `RampStatus` early and often.** The fastest way to lose a sales team's trust is a leaderboard that ranks a rep who started three weeks ago. One filter, and you are the person who thought about it.
