## What this section will cover

What a GTM engineer actually builds in Salesforce for a B2B training company. The motion, the metric tree, the dashboards that get opened every Monday — and the ones that get abandoned. Built end to end on CRM Analytics Academy's own data.

## Planned lessons

- **GTM Engineering** — What a GTM engineer actually builds in Salesforce for a B2B training company. The motion, the metric tree, the dashboards that get opened every Monday — and the ones that get abandoned. Built end to end on CRM Analytics Academy's own data.
- **Meet the Academy** — CRM Analytics Academy as a company: six offerings across three pricing models, one-off classroom revenue, five segments, two sales motions, and the specific analytics problems each one creates.
- **The Data Model** — The twenty-one tables behind every dashboard in this section, what grain each one sits at, and the four relationships that have to hold or none of the numbers reconcile.
- **Load the Warehouse** — Get all twenty-one Academy CSVs into CRM Analytics: upload order, the field metadata that has to be right before the first chart, and the three recipes that do the joining once so no dashboard has to.
- **Grain & Fan-Out** — The single most expensive bug in GTM analytics: a one-to-many join that silently multiplies revenue. How it happens, how to see it in ten seconds, and the four safe patterns.
- **Metric Contracts** — Most GTM analytics arguments are definition arguments wearing a data costume. How to write a metric down so marketing and sales get the same number, and which the Academy metrics have no single right answer.
- **Designing for Decisions** — Why most GTM dashboards get opened twice and abandoned, the four-question brief that prevents it, and a layout pattern that works for every dashboard in this section.
- **The Metric Tree** — One diagram connecting an assistant's answer to an ARR number. Where each of the ten dashboards in this section sits on it, and why ARR is a lagging indicator of something you measured four months ago.
- **Interview Questions** — Rehearse the GTM analytics interview — grain and fan-out, metric definitions, seat versus prepaid-pool retention, leading indicators, and how to push back on a dashboard request without sounding obstructive.
