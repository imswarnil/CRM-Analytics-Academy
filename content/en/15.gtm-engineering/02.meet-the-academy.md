# Meet the Academy

The company in every dashboard from here on is **CRM Analytics Academy** — the business behind this course, modelled as a company. It sells this same training four ways: to individual learners who want to get certified, to companies upskilling their admins and analysts, in classrooms at six training centers, and as implementation projects for teams that would rather have it built. About 140 customer accounts, 2,800 leads, $13.4M closed to date, and roughly $7.0M of ARR by September 2026.

The numbers are synthetic. The shape is not: it is the shape of a real B2B training business, and each awkward detail exists because it breaks a metric you would otherwise trust.

And one revenue stream that is not in that table at all: **classroom batches** sold seat by seat to individuals and companies, recorded in `batches.csv` and `enrollments.csv`. About $3.3M of real money over eighteen months, and none of it is ARR.

That mix is the whole reason this section exists. Watch what happens to one innocent metric:

::tip{icon="i-lucide-lightbulb"}
**The interview answer.** Asked "how would you measure retention here", the weak answer is a formula. The strong one: *"Which offering? Seat plans retain on a renewal date, prepaid pools retain only if they are used up, the programme fee retains or doesn't, and the classroom does not retain at all — it is repeat purchase. I'd build retention for the first three, a repeat-booking rate for the classroom, and I'd label any blend clearly."*
::

## The cast

You will filter by these people constantly, so they are worth knowing.

::field-table
---
dataset: reps.csv
grain: one row per rep, 40 rows
fields:
  - name: "AE"
    type: "14 reps"
    role: "dimension"
    note: "Carry quota. Segment-aligned, so comparing across segments is unfair by default."
  - name: "SDR / BDR"
    type: "13 reps"
    role: "dimension"
    note: "SDR works inbound — free-course learners and quote requests. BDR works outbound to L&D leaders. Different jobs, different metrics."
  - name: "SC"
    type: "6 reps"
    role: "dimension"
    note: "Solution consultants — they design the pilot cohort and the curriculum, which is why pilot length is measurable."
  - name: "CSM"
    type: "7 reps"
    role: "dimension"
    note: "Own the installed base and chase seat activation. Join to arr_snapshots to see a book of business."
  - name: "RampStatus"
    type: "Ramping / Partial / Full"
    role: "dimension"
    note: "The fairness filter. A rep in month two on a quota chart makes the whole chart a lie."
---
::

::tip{icon="i-lucide-users"}
**Use `RampStatus` early and often.** The fastest way to lose a sales team's trust is a leaderboard that ranks a rep who started three weeks ago. One filter, and you are the person who thought about it.
::
