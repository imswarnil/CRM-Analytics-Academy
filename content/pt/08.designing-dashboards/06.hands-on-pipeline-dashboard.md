# O Painel de Tubulação

Noventa minutos. Seis widgets, três recusas, e os dois cheques que vêm antes de qualquer um vê-lo.

::build-brief
---
dashboard: "Pipeline Health"
audience: "The VP of Sales and four regional directors"
question: "Is the quarter covered, and which stage needs attention this week?"
decision: "Where the leadership team spends this week's attention — which stage, which region, which deals"
cadence: "Opened Monday morning; reviewed in the weekly pipeline call"
success: "Coverage readable in four seconds, and the stuck-deal table short enough to act on the same day"
datasets:
  - "opportunities.csv"
  - "accounts.csv"
  - "reps.csv"
notInScope:
  - "Rep leaderboards. The directors asked; the answer is no on this screen, because a dashboard that ranks people gets read for the ranking and the pipeline question stops being asked"
  - "Forecast. Forecasting is Build 13 in the RevOps section and it is a different discipline — putting a forecast beside raw pipeline invites people to treat pipeline as a forecast"
  - "Closed-won revenue. This screen is about unsold pipeline; mixing sold and unsold on one dashboard is the most common way an exec screen misleads"
---
::

## As métricas, especificadas

::metric-spec
---
items:
  - name: "Open Pipeline"
    formula: "sum(Amount) where the opportunity is open and CloseDate falls in the selected quarter"
    grain: "one value per filter selection"
    source: "opportunities.csv"
    watchout: "Open means not closed-won AND not closed-lost. Writing it as 'StageName != Closed Won' silently keeps every closed-lost deal in the pipeline, and the number looks healthy for exactly the wrong reason."
  - name: "Coverage"
    formula: "Open Pipeline ÷ quarter target"
    grain: "one value per filter selection"
    source: "opportunities.csv, with the target as a fixed figure"
    watchout: "The target must not facet with the selection. Filter to EMEA and the pipeline narrows correctly while the company target stays put — which is why this widget has faceting off and its denominator stated in the subtitle."
  - name: "Days in Stage"
    formula: "today − the date the opportunity entered its current stage"
    grain: "one value per opportunity"
    source: "opportunities.csv"
    watchout: "Needs a stage-entry date. If the dataset only carries the last-modified date, this measures editing activity rather than stage movement — a deal someone touched yesterday looks fresh while having sat in the same stage for four months."
---
::

## Os dois cheques antes da expedição

::lesson-steps
---
items:
  - title: "Contra a chave de resposta"
    description: "O README declara o verdadeiro oleoduto aberto da Academia. Um múltiplo limpo muito alto é um ventilador, baixo geralmente significa um filtro que exclui nulos junto com os valores que você quis dizer. Não navegue em 'perto o suficiente'."
  - title: "Como outra pessoa"
    description: "Abra como o usuário restrito da seção de acesso. Eles podem ver isso, e é o que eles veem? Sair de uma conta de administração torna todos os erros de segurança invisíveis para precisamente a pessoa responsável por isso."
---
::

::tip{icon="i-lucide-message-square-quote"}
**Adicione o widget de definições antes de compartilhá-lo, não depois do primeiro argumento.** Um widget de texto carregando as três fórmulas acima e o último tempo de atualização responde as únicas duas perguntas que alguém faz na primeira semana - "por que esse número é diferente do meu" e "é isso ao vivo" - e ele transforma ambos de um pedido de apoio em um olhar.
::
