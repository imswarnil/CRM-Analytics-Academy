# ビルド: サービス、SLA、デフレ

**ビルド 16.** サポートは、ここでの収益メトリックであり、このビルドは、フラットなバージョンが利用可能でより容易であるときに正直に物事を測定することについてほとんどです。

::build-brief
---
dashboard: "Service, SLA & Deflection"
audience: "The VP of Support, with the CRO as a monthly reader"
question: "Are we meeting SLA on the accounts that matter, was any spike our fault, and is Ask AI deflection real?"
decision: "Where to add support headcount, and whether to expand the AI assistant's scope"
cadence: "Weekly for the support team; monthly in the revenue review"
success: "A support problem can be shown to have revenue consequences, with account-level evidence"
datasets:
  - "cases.csv"
  - "csat_surveys.csv"
  - "platform_health_daily.csv"
  - "accounts.csv"
notInScope:
  - "Individual agent performance. Ranking agents on a dashboard that also reports SLA turns a diagnostic screen into an evaluative one, and the diagnosis stops being trusted by the people who have to report the data"
  - "Consumption risk. That is Build 15 — a support problem and a consumption problem need different interventions"
---
::

## メトリック

::metric-spec
---
items:
  - name: "SLA Attainment"
    formula: "count(cases where ResolutionHours <= SLATargetHours) ÷ count(closed cases)"
    grain: "per support tier per month"
    source: "cases.csv"
    watchout: "Verify the SLABreached flag against ResolutionHours and SLATargetHours before trusting it. Also decide explicitly whether still-open cases past target count as breached — excluding them flatters the number precisely when a backlog is building, which is the moment the metric most needs to be honest."
  - name: "ARR Behind Breaches"
    formula: "sum(distinct account ARR) over accounts with at least one breach in the period"
    grain: "per month"
    source: "cases.csv joined to accounts.csv"
    watchout: "Distinct at the account level. An account with nine breaches would otherwise contribute its ARR nine times — the fan-out from the datasets and modelling section, arriving in a service dashboard where nobody expects it."
  - name: "AI Deflection Rate (net)"
    formula: "count(DeflectedByAI = true AND Reopened = false) ÷ count(all cases)"
    grain: "per month per product"
    source: "cases.csv"
    watchout: "Show gross and net side by side. Reporting only gross is the flattering version and it is the version that gets a team's AI investment renewed on a false premise — and then quietly blamed when support load does not fall."
  - name: "Platform-Attributable Volume"
    formula: "case count on days where HadIncident = true, compared with the trailing daily median"
    grain: "per day per product"
    source: "cases.csv and platform_health_daily.csv, joined on date and product"
    watchout: "Join on date and product only. platform_health_daily has no account key, and inventing one — or faceting it by an account selection — implies a customer-level relationship the data cannot support."
---
::
