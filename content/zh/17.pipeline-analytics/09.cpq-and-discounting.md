# 构建:CPQ、贴现和交易服务台

目前为止每个仪表板都是为了交易 这个是关于 **他们花了你多少钱** ——这是大多数公司从未建造过的仪表板,正是这个原因,比值会悄悄泄露多年.

::build-brief
---
dashboard: "CPQ & Discount Governance"
audience: "Deal desk, RevOps, VP Sales, and finance quarterly"
cadence: "Monthly, and at every pricing review"
question: "Where are we giving away margin, and what is the approval process costing us in cycle time?"
decision: "Which discount thresholds change, and which approval steps get removed."
success: "Average discount stops drifting upward; approval days fall without discount rising."
datasets:
  - "quotes.csv"
  - "opportunities.csv"
  - "opportunity_products.csv"
  - "products.csv"
notInScope:
  - "Individual rep discounting league tables — that creates gaming, not discipline"
---
::

## 使这成为可能的版本决定

::note{icon="i-lucide-file-stack"}
`quotes.csv` 保留 **每个版本**不只是那个被接受的 795列横跨~386机会. 覆盖引文的系统可以告诉你卖什么;只有版本的才能告诉你 **你怎么会到那里** 谈判的道路就是模式的所在。
::

::metric-spec
---
items:
  - name: "Discount Creep"
    formula: "avg(DiscountPct) by Version number"
    grain: "Per version, across all quotes"
    source: "quotes.csv — all versions, not just final"
    watchout: "This is the one analysis where you must NOT filter IsFinalVersion. Every other chart on the dashboard should. Mixing the two is the main way this dashboard goes wrong."
  - name: "Realised Discount"
    formula: "(ListTotal − NetTotal) / ListTotal, final version only"
    grain: "Per opportunity"
    source: "quotes.csv where IsFinalVersion = true"
    watchout: "Only meaningful on won deals — a rejected quote's discount was never given. Filter to the opportunity's IsWon = true or you are averaging in prices nobody ever paid."
  - name: "Approval Cost in Days"
    formula: "avg(ApprovalDays) by discount band"
    grain: "Per band"
    source: "quotes.csv"
    watchout: "Approval days sit inside the sales cycle, so they are a velocity cost as well as an overhead. A 35% discount approved over 11 days costs the discount AND the eleven days. Nobody counts the second half."
---
::

## 这个仪表盘的四项发现

::lesson-slides
---
items:
  - kicker: "Finding 1"
    title: "每个版本的折扣蠕动"
    icon: "i-lucide-trending-up"
    bullets:
      - "~2.5 points per re-quote"
      - "A 4-version deal ends ~10 points worse"
      - "Fix: cap versions, or require approval to re-quote"
      - "Invisible without version history"
  - kicker: "Finding 2"
    title: "贴现可能赢不了交易"
    icon: "i-lucide-help-circle"
    bullets:
      - "Plot win rate by discount band"
      - "Often flat, or an inverted U"
      - "Heavy discounts cluster on deals already lost"
      - "Correlation, so state the caveat"
  - kicker: "Finding 3"
    title: "批准日为纯周期成本"
    icon: "i-lucide-hourglass"
    bullets:
      - "~6 days at 15%, ~11 at 40%+"
      - "Sits inside the sales cycle"
      - "A velocity lever nobody counts"
  - kicker: "Finding 4"
    title: "微薄的差值最低"
    icon: "i-lucide-alert-triangle"
    bullets:
      - "Certification Vouchers: 35% gross margin"
      - "Discounted like the 84% Team Plan"
      - "Join products.csv to see real margin after discount"
      - "Some lines may be sold below cost"
---
::

::tip{icon="i-lucide-calculator"}
**找到4就是升级** 每行计算贴现后毛比值: `NetUnitPrice` 与产品相对 `GrossMarginPct`。 。 。 对于35%的边际产品, 25%的折扣大约为13%, 而对于学院以固定价格从考试供应商手中购买的票券, 这是一个应该有意识的决定,而不是谈判的副作用。
::

::note{icon="i-lucide-users"}
**把这个仪表板设定为过程 而不是人** 一个折扣领导板 产生代表谁隐藏折扣 以非标准条件, 而你失去了可见 的东西 你试图治理。 例外表的存在是为了检验政策是否符合现实——当你提出时大声说出来。
