# 构建:执行GTM 董事会

**建设17号楼.** 九数,一屏,无卷. 这是课程中最难的课,其内容比任何其他建筑都少。

::build-brief
---
dashboard: "Academy Analytics Board"
audience: "The CEO, CRO, CFO and CMO, monthly"
question: "Is the go-to-market machine working, and where is it not?"
decision: "Where the next quarter's money and headcount go"
cadence: "Monthly, in the exec review — on screen, in the room"
success: "A viewer who has never seen it can state the company's situation in four seconds, and the meeting argues about what to do rather than about whose number is right"
datasets:
  - "arr_snapshots.csv"
  - "usage_monthly.csv"
  - "opp_enriched"
  - "marketing_spend.csv"
  - "accounts.csv"
notInScope:
  - "Every rep, campaign, keyword, case and account detail — ~120 of the ~130 widgets built across Builds 1–16. Each one lives on its own dashboard and is one click away"
  - "Anything nobody would act on this quarter. A number that cannot change a decision in the next ninety days is a number for a different meeting"
  - "Anything that needs a paragraph to interpret. If it cannot be read in a glance it will be misread in a glance"
---
::

## 为什么最小的仪表板最难

::note{icon="i-lucide-scissors"}
**你带着大约130个部件来到这里 横跨16个仪表板, 每个人都有个人 谁建造和关心它。** 九生灭. 这栋大楼的工作不是选择这9座大楼——大多数人会选择一个类似的9座大楼——它能够对建设竞选-表现屏幕的人说,为什么他们的工作不在董事会,为什么这不是对其价值的判断。 这种对话是整个课程所培养的高级技能。
::

      - "Readable without interpretation"
      - "Explained by something else on the screen"

      - "Necessarily coarse — the detail is one click away"
    verdict: "Nine of these exist. Not sixteen."
  - title: "离开董事会"
    subtitle: "Failed it"
    icon: "i-lucide-x"
    pros:
      - "Often genuinely important to its own team"
    cons:
      - "Downstream of a number already shown"
      - "Too slow to act on in a quarter"
      - "Needs a paragraph, so it will be misread in a glance"
    verdict: "Link to it. Do not shrink it onto this screen."
---

## 当有人问为什么他们的部件不在时,该怎么说?

::tip{icon="i-lucide-handshake"}
**"这个板子回答了一个问题——是机器在起作用——你回答的问题比那个要好,所以它得到了自己的屏幕而不是这个屏幕的角落".** 确实,这不是一个刷子, 它通常结束好的对话。 不起作用的是同意添加它"只是为了这个季度",因为一个14个数字的板子在4秒内不能被读取,没有人提议删除一个数字.
::
