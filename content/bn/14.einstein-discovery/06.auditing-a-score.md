walkthrough:
  org: "Analytics Studio, Academy Analytics app, with leads.csv, opportunities.csv and accounts.csv loaded"
  shots:
    - shot: "State what the score claims"
      screen: "leads.csv → the LeadScore field"
      say: "Before testing anything, write down what the score is supposed to mean. A lead score claims that higher-scoring leads convert more often. That is a testable claim, and almost nobody tests it — the score arrives with a vendor or a previous admin and is trusted because it exists."
      onscreen: "A score is a claim. Test it."
      seconds: 40
    - shot: "Bucket the score"
      screen: "Lens on leads → bucket LeadScore into bands of ten → group by band"
      say: "Bucket the score into bands and count leads in each. You are establishing the shape before looking at outcomes — if ninety percent of leads sit in two bands, the score has very little resolution to offer no matter how well it predicts."
      onscreen: "Check the spread first"
      seconds: 40
    - shot: "Join what actually happened"
      screen: "Join leads to opportunities on ConvertedOpportunityId → conversion flag per lead"
      say: "Bring in the outcome: did this lead become an opportunity, and did that opportunity close won. Now each band can be measured against reality rather than against the score's own confidence."
      seconds: 45
    - shot: "The test"
      screen: "Chart conversion rate by score band"
      say: "Conversion rate by band. If the score works, this climbs steadily from left to right. If the line is flat, the score is noise wearing a number. If it climbs and then flattens above seventy, the score distinguishes bad from average and tells you nothing about the top — which is precisely where the sales team is spending its time."
      onscreen: "Does the line climb?"
      seconds: 55
    - shot: "Compare against a trivial baseline"
      screen: "Conversion rate by lead source, beside the score bands"
      say: "Now the honest comparison. Chart conversion by source alongside conversion by score band. If source separates outcomes better than the score does, the score is adding nothing over a field you already had — and that is a result worth reporting, however uncomfortable."
      onscreen: "Beat the simplest alternative, or admit it"
      seconds: 50
    - shot: "Check the volume behind each band"
      screen: "Put the lead count on the same chart"
      say: "Put counts beside the rates. A band with nine leads showing a sixty percent conversion rate is a coincidence, and it will be quoted as evidence the score works. Rates without volumes are how a bad score survives an audit."
      onscreen: "Rates need their counts"
      seconds: 40
    - shot: "Do the same for the health score"
      screen: "accounts.csv → HealthScore → against contraction in arr_snapshots"
      say: "Repeat the whole thing for the account health score, using contraction and churn in the A-R-R snapshots as the outcome. Same method, different table. This is the audit that matters most commercially, because health scores drive renewal attention and nobody ever checks them."
      onscreen: "Health score vs what happened"
      seconds: 50
    - shot: "Write down the finding"
      screen: "Notes — one paragraph"
      say: "Write the result in one paragraph: what the score claims, what the test showed, and what you recommend. Keep the score, keep it with a caveat, or replace it. A finding nobody writes down changes nothing, and this particular finding tends to be unwelcome enough that it needs to exist in writing."
      seconds: 40

# একটি স্কোর পর্যালোচনা করা হচ্ছে

একাডেমির কার্যক্রম চালিয়ে যাবে `LeadScore` অতঃপর কর ্ ম বন ্ টনকারী ফেরেশতাগণের , `HealthScore`. . দুটোই বিশ্বস্ত। ( ১ করি. এই শিক্ষাটা হল যে, এই বিভাগে সবচেয়ে বেশি স্থানান্তর করা ঘন্টা — যে - সমস্ত কোম্পানীতে আপনি কাজ করেন, তাদের কাছে এই ধরনের একটা স্কোর থাকবে ।

::note{icon="i-lucide-search-check"}
**একটি স্কোর একটি দাবি: আরো বেশি অর্থ আছে।** এটা আসলে যা ঘটেছে তার পরীক্ষা এবং এক ঘন্টার মধ্যে পরীক্ষা করা যায়। যেহেতু এটা খুব কমই চালানো কঠিন, তাই এটি একটি বিক্রেতা বা অন্য কোথাও যাওয়ার প্রশাসনের কাছে আসে, দুই বছর ধরে এটা ড্যাশবোর্ডে এসেছে এবং প্রশ্ন করা হচ্ছে যে, যারা এটাকে ব্যবহার করে তাদের প্রশ্ন করা হচ্ছে।
::
